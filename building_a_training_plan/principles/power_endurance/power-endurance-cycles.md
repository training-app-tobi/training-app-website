# Power Endurance Cycles: Where They Fit and How Much

**Type:** Principles, Planning · **Trains:** Power endurance

**TL;DR:** Training is polarized between really high-intensity bouldering and power on one side and a lot of easy, sustainable volume on the other, but close to a performance phase you still have to go into the power endurance zone. Recover enough between intervals that each one is still done with high power output, instead of digging a deeper and deeper hole. Run a cycle of 6–8 progressive sessions, no more than two a week, alongside intensive bouldering or easy endurance. Power endurance builds and fades quickly, so ramp it up when you need it and keep it simmering the rest of the year.

## Where power endurance fits
- Training is polarized: really high-intensity bouldering and explosive power, plus a lot of volume at lower, sustainable intensities. Because of the nature of climbing, you still have to go into the power endurance zone when you are coming close to a performance phase.
- Power endurance (anaerobic output) work means high-intensity, longer-duration efforts that make you fatigued and pumped. The key is to recover enough between intervals that the following interval can still be done with a high power output.
- **Warning:** A common mistake: in something like a 4x4 workout you dig a deeper and deeper hole, and the last few boulder problems are very low quality and very low difficulty, so you are not training endurance at the level you want to perform at.

## Programming a power endurance cycle
| Element | Do this |
|---|---|
| Total sessions | 6–8 power endurance sessions |
| Frequency | No more than two per week, sometimes only one per week |
| Other sessions in the week | Intensive bouldering or low-intensity endurance |
| Result | Over 6–8 progressive sessions at 1–2 per week, power endurance ramps up: it shows in the intensive triples, and at the end of the cycle on the rock |

- Power endurance is a transient factor: it builds up quickly and degrades quickly, and is very hard to maintain, but this is not a concern. Ramp it up when you have a specific need for it; the rest of the year keep it simmering with a little of this training once every few weeks, while developing power in the bouldering gym or building aerobic endurance.

## How much power endurance
- Be careful how much power endurance you do: only enough to improve your performance on rock. Power endurance cycles don't need to be long, and every cycle, even a focused power endurance block, must also include high-intensity bouldering and plenty of easy movement to maintain aerobic ability and a high level of power.
