# Finger Strength vs Climbing Grade: Climbing Harder With Weak Fingers

**Type:** Principles · **Trains:** Finger strength · **For:** weak fingers for your grade

**TL;DR:** Finger strength is a good indicator of how you might perform on rock, but it doesn't decide whether you get up a particular climb. With weak fingers for your grade, other qualities let you perform at your grade (aerobic capacity and resting skill, kneebars, creative rests and micro beta), but stay consistent: sometimes you just need to pull really hard.

## Finger strength and grade
- Finger strength is a good indicator of how you might perform on rock, but it doesn't tell you for certain whether you will get up a particular climb.
- With weak fingers for your grade (fingers that may never be the strongest), find other ways to perform at your grade: aerobic capacity and optimal resting time (in the note Aerobic Capacity and Resting Time When Your Fingers Are Weak), and kneebars, creative rests and micro beta (in the note Kneebars, Creative Rests and Micro Beta When Your Fingers Are Weak).

## Consistency
- **Consistency:** consistency is key to making good strength gains. You can work around weak finger strength, but sometimes you just need to pull really hard; consistent hard work works.
