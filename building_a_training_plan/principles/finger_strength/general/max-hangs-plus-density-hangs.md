# Max Hangs Plus Density Hangs: Deloads and Grip Types

**Type:** Principles, Planning · **Trains:** Finger strength, Recovery

**TL;DR:** Max hangs twice a week plus density hangs once a week tries to do two things at once and shortchanges both protocols, leaving little finger capacity for anything else; with such a protocol, adding about 2 kg a week, deload every 4 weeks. You can max hang several grip types at once: up to five is fine, but the overlap risk is present at four and very high above five.

## Max hangs plus density hangs: deloads and grip types
- **Warning:** Max hangs twice a week plus density hangs once a week is trying to do two different things at once and shortchanges both protocols, because they are not very symbiotic: one day the tissue is highly fatigued, another day it is hit at full intensity. Three such days a week on top of climbing leaves little finger capacity for anything else, unless you have great capacity.
- With a protocol like this (max hangs twice a week, density hangs once a week, adding about 2 kg every week), deload every 4 weeks.
- You can max hang several grip types at once; there is no need to focus on one first. Up to five is fine, and more is possible, but the overlap risk is very high above five, and present even at four, though four is still okay.
