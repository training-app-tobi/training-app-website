# Training the Fatigability of Finger Strength for Bouldering

**Type:** Principles · **Trains:** Finger strength, Power endurance, Endurance · **For:** Boulderers

**TL;DR:** Finger strength is extremely important for boulderers, but also train the fatigability of high-end force production. If you feel strong for 10–20 s but flail after about 30 s, this is lacking: anaerobic capacity (AnCap) work, long-duration isometrics and appropriate aerobic capacity (AeroCap) work have a big impact.

## Finger strength
- Finger strength is extremely important, but also train the fatigability of high-end force production.
- Sign that this is lacking: you feel strong for 10–20 s but flail after about 30 s.
- Anaerobic capacity (AnCap) work, long-duration isometrics and appropriate aerobic capacity (AeroCap) work have a big impact here.
