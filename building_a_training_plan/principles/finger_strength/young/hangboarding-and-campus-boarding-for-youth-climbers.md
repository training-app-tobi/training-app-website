## Hangboarding and campus boarding for youth climbers
- **Warning:** epiphyseal (growth plate) stress fractures are more likely to occur from campus-board-type movements because they are dynamic.
- These movements are not as dynamic as climbing itself. If youths do hard boulders, fingerboarding and probably campus boarding are preparatory: if they can't handle those, they should not be doing hard boulder problems.
- Building tolerance in advance makes you likely to avoid injuries and do harder things safely.
- The concern that coaches over-prescribe is reasonable and probably realistic, but bad programming can hurt anyone. Fingerboarding is about as safe as possible relative to such stressful interventions.
- Campus boarding with competent instruction, as part of a reasonably balanced plan, should be educational and protective: it teaches good motor patterns and acclimates the body to those forces in a relatively controlled way, compared with the uncontrolled, hard-to-assess intensity of bouldering itself.
- **Warning:** take care with the amount of campusing you program and supervise kids; don't just send a 9-year-old to smash around on the campus board.
