# One Goal Per Training Cycle

**Type:** Planning, Principles · **Trains:** General strength, Power, Endurance

**TL;DR:** Strength, power and endurance are best built independently, one at a time; once built to a certain level, they can be maintained together. Give each 4-week training phase one number one priority: do it first in every session, do it fresh, and measure the program's success by it. Switch the priority month to month.

## Build first, then maintain
- Maintaining all the different facets of your climbing at once is possible, but only after you've developed them.
- Strength, power and endurance each need to be built, and they are best built independently. Once each is built to a certain level, you can maintain it.

## One priority per phase
- For each 4-week training phase, draw a simple graphic for yourself (a target or a pyramid, the shape doesn't matter) with your number one priority in the center, then number two and number three around it.

- Priority 1 is always done first in the session.
- Priority 1 is always done when you're fresh (e.g. after a rest day).
- Priority 1 is the metric you measure the success of the training program by.

- Having a whole bunch of priorities is the same as having none.
- Example: "this month I work on finger strength". Doing a little endurance and a little weight training alongside is okay, but finger strength is the key.
- Every training phase needs one thing you're measuring and one thing you're trying to manage.
- Focus on one thing and switch it month to month to month and you'll start making progress. Focus on a whole bunch of different things and you'll end up in the same place you are right now.
