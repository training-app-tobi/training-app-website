# Energy Systems as Gears: Power, Endurance and Duration

**Type:** Principles · **Trains:** Power, Power endurance, Endurance

**TL;DR:** First gear is the anaerobic alactic (ATP-CP) system: high power, low endurance, up to about 12 seconds; second gear is anaerobic lactic (glycolytic): medium power and endurance, from 12 to about 90 seconds; third to fifth gears are aerobic oxidative: low power, high endurance, 90+ seconds. All three fuel all activity; one predominates only when you go at near-maximum effort for those durations, so intensity decides which system you train.

## Energy systems as gears

| Gear | Energy system | Power | Endurance | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| First | Anaerobic alactic (ATP-CP) | High | Low | Up to about 12 seconds |
| Second | Anaerobic lactic (glycolytic) | Medium | Medium | 12 seconds to about 90 seconds |
| Third to fifth | Aerobic oxidative | Low | High | 90+ seconds |

- All three systems fuel all activity and all are used at all times; one predominates depending on duration and intensity.
- Intensity is the big factor: you mainly use a given system only if you go at near-maximum effort for the durations above. Walking slowly for 30 seconds barely taps second gear; you're probably in the oxidative zone. This matters when assigning training efforts.
