# Progressive Overload Is Underrated

**Type:** Principles

**TL;DR:** Many climbers, even enthusiastic, progress-oriented ones, don't think about progressive overload specifically, yet you won't get better without some form of it, accidental or intentional. The same holds in rehab: increasing weight, reps and sets keeps challenging the tissue and drives both strength and injury recovery.

## Progressive overload
- Underrated: many climbers, even enthusiastic, progress-oriented ones, don't think about it specifically.
- You won't get better without some form of progressive overload, accidental or intentional.
- The same holds in rehab: increase weight, reps and sets of exercises to continually challenge the tissue. Progressive overload drives improvement both in strength and in injury recovery.
