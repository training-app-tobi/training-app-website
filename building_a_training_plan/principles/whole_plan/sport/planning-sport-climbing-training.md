# Planning Sport Climbing Training: Pacing and Recruitment

**Type:** Planning, Principles · **Trains:** Endurance, Finger strength · **For:** Sport climbers

**TL;DR:** Sport climbing needs a blend of endurance and strength. Plan the pacing of your training deliberately (1 move per 10 seconds and 3 moves per 10 seconds are very different experiences), and counter the strength loss from high volume with just the right amount of complementary high-intensity work in the sport peaking phase.

## Overall
- Sport climbing needs a blend of endurance and strength, and many sport climbers find this mixture (or conflict) tricky to plan for.

## Pacing
- Plan the pacing of your training deliberately.
- 1 move per 10 seconds and 3 moves per 10 seconds are significantly different experiences physiologically, technically and psychologically.
- Slow climbers especially should take note.

## Recruitment
- Sport climbing training often involves lots of volume, which typically has a derecruitment effect on strength.
- Because this is mainly a neurological adaptation, counter it with just the right amount of complementary high-intensity work in the sport peaking phase, so you feel both strong and fit.
