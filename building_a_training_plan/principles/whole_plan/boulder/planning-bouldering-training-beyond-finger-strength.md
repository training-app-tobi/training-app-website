# Planning Bouldering Training: Beyond Finger Strength

**Type:** Planning, Principles · **Trains:** Finger strength, Antagonists & core, Mobility · **For:** Boulderers

**TL;DR:** Many boulderers put about 99% of their training focus on finger strength and about 1% on everything else; this is not ideal. Plan attention for three areas: finger strength including its fatigability, end-to-end tension for steep terrain, and mobility and flexibility, and individualise the plan.

## Overall balance
- Many boulderers put about 99% of their training focus on finger strength and about 1% on everything else; this is not ideal. Plan attention for three areas as well: finger strength, end-to-end tension, mobility and flexibility (each has its own note: Training the Fatigability of Finger Strength for Bouldering, End-to-End Tension for Steep Bouldering, and Mobility and Flexibility for Boulderers).
- These are broad patterns seen across boulderers; individualising the plan is always very important.
