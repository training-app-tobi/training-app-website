# Power Quality: Train Power Fresh, With Positive Impacts

**Type:** Principles · **Trains:** Power, Finger strength

**TL;DR:** Train power fresh, potentiated and with low reps of high-quality, high-speed positive impacts: warm the fingers, activate with one or two max hangs or recruitment pulls, then get on climbs you can hit hard and fast and stick. Limit boulders that are too hard give no real positive impacts, so after one limit boulder, drop the grade by one and own climbs.

## Training power
- When training power, the number one focus is quality: quality, speed and explosiveness. Do it when fresh: start the session, get really warmed up and get potentiated.
- Getting ready for finger power: do something light and really fast, then follow the sequence below. Most people don't do the last step well enough.

| Step | Do this |
|---|---|
| 1. Warm the fingers | A series of hangs, or the boards or some boulders |
| 2. Activate | One or two max hangs or recruitment pulls, just to get activated |
| 3. Power climbing | Get on climbs that are all high-speed, making positive impacts |

- A positive impact means hitting a hold with quality: you stick the hold, you land it, and then you can keep moving, or you keep it. Too often people just hit holds and blow off.
- Power training is all about low reps and high quality; the same goes for max strength. You are looking for quality, high-speed impacts.
- **Warning:** Many people train power with limit bouldering but pick climbs that are too hard, so they don't get real positive impacts. A limit boulder is like playing roulette: you squeeze and try as hard as you can, but you have to pick the right climb, one that lets you stick really hard moves.

| Drill step | Do this |
|---|---|
| 1 | One limit boulder |
| 2 | While still fresh, drop the grade by one and own climbs: hit holds really hard and really fast |

- It is like a great campus board session, where you don't really fall: you stick everything and hit things hard. Same with RFD (velocity) pulls, where every rep succeeds and you never fail. The best way to get power is to actually stick the holds and go through the full contractions.
