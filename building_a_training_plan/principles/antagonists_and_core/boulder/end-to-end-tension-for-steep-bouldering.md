# End-to-End Tension for Steep Bouldering

**Type:** Principles, Exercise/protocol · **Trains:** Antagonists & core · **For:** Boulderers

**TL;DR:** Much bouldering at the higher end of the grade scale happens on steeper terrain, which needs high levels of coordinated activation of the posterior chain together with the abs, pectorals, hip flexors and biceps. Train it with strength drills on the wall, TRX or rings work at full extension, and floor work such as modified glute bridges.

## End-to-end tension
- Much bouldering at the higher end of the grade scale happens on steeper terrain. This needs high levels of coordinated activation of the posterior chain together with the muscles associated with steep terrain (abs, pectorals, hip flexors, biceps, etc.).

| Exercise type | Example |
|---|---|
| On the wall | Strength drills on the wall |
| Suspension | TRX/rings work at full extension |
| Floor work | Modified glute bridges |
