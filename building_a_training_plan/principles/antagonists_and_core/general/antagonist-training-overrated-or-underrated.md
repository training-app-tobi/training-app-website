# Antagonist Training: What Is Overrated and What Is Underrated

**Type:** Principles · **Trains:** Antagonists & core

**TL;DR:** The muscles called antagonists are essential for climbing performance, so training them is underrated, but calling it "antagonist training" is misleading. Expecting specific antagonist exercises to solve injury problems is mostly overrated, while regimented, overloaded push training (not just some push-ups from time to time) is probably underrated: most climbers don't do it.

## Antagonist training
- The muscles called "antagonists" are extremely useful and essential for climbing performance, so in that sense training them is underrated; calling it "antagonist training" for climbing is misleading.
- Expecting specific antagonist exercises to solve injury problems is overrated: using antagonist work for injury prevention or rehab is not always inappropriate, but it is mostly overrated.
- Push training done in a regimented, overloaded way (as opposed to doing some push-ups from time to time) is probably underrated: most climbers don't do it.
