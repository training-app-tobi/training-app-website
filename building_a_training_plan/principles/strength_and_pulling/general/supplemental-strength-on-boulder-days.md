## Supplemental strength on boulder days

- Resistance to weight training for climbing can generally be traced to one of two thinking traps: (1) a climber got this good by just climbing, so believes no other training, especially non-climbing training, could help; (2) a climber pictures bodybuilders when imagining weights, not understanding that bodybuilding is very different from strength training.
- Supplemental strength training is done to prevent injury, to build a strong base of training capacity and to enhance the training process.
- Climbers can add a little strength training into a climbing day rather than dedicating a whole separate session to it.
- Three session formats that combine bouldering, supplemental strength and skill work (Brick-Style Training, Integrated Boulder and Skill-Strategy-Strength) are in the note Boulder, Strength and Skill Sessions.
