# Tips for Weight Training: Learn to Lift, Test, Use Eccentrics and Focus on a Few Exercises

**Type:** Principles · **Trains:** General strength

**TL;DR:** Weight training targets specific muscles with basic movements and makes progressive loading easy, but training in hypertrophy and strength ranges is high intensity, so learn to lift correctly first. Test every 4–6 weeks, use eccentric movements first when pushing the load or getting past plateaus (eccentric strength declines more slowly with age), and focus on 2–3 exercises done consistently rather than everything at once.

## Tips for weight training
- Weight training targets specific muscles with basic movements and makes progressive loading easy.
- Working within the right sets, reps and intensity is important to get muscle synthesis and strength adaptations.
- **Warning:** Training in hypertrophy and strength ranges is high intensity: learn to lift correctly and get comfortable with weight-based exercises before pushing the training.
- Schedule testing sessions about every 4–6 weeks to check whether the strength training is working.
- With age, eccentric (lengthening) strength declines more slowly than concentric (shortening) strength, so consider using eccentric movements first when pushing the training load or getting past plateaus.
- Don't spread yourself too thin: rather than trying to strengthen all muscles at once, focus on 2–3 exercises and do them consistently.
- You can never start strength training too early.
