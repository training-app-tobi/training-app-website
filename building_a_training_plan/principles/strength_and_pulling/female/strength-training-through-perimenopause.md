# Strength Training Through Perimenopause

**Type:** Principles · **Trains:** General strength · **For:** Female, perimenopause

**TL;DR:** The decline of estrogen during the perimenopause, which can last up to 10 years and may be felt from the early 40s, coincides with a loss of muscle mass, strength and bone density. Climbers approaching this stage usually already have a good foundation of climbing experience, so it is a good time to add off-the-wall weight training to build and maintain muscle mass (see the note Tips for Weight Training).

## What the perimenopause is
- The menopause is an age-dependent physiological change caused by the decline of estrogen and progesterone, which ends the menstrual cycle.
- The period over which these hormones decline is the perimenopause and can last up to 10 years.
- Onset age varies between individuals; climbers may start to feel changes from their early 40s.
- Estrogen is essential for building muscle and bone, so its decline during the menopause coincides with a reduction in muscle mass, strength and bone density.

## Why add weight training
- Weight training is a good way to build and maintain muscle mass.
- Climbers approaching this stage usually already have a good foundation of climbing experience, so it is a good time to add off-the-wall weighted exercises.
