# Menopause and Building Muscle

**Type:** Principles · **Trains:** General strength · **For:** Female, menopause

**TL;DR:** At the menopause, estrogen declines and its anabolic effects are lost, so it can be harder to create new muscle; the anabolic resistance that comes with age adds to this (see the note Ageing and Anabolic Resistance). Prioritise some strength work: resistance exercises at least 2–3 times per week, focusing on compound exercises that work multiple muscle groups.

## Menopause: declining estrogen
- As female athletes get older, developing strength may become harder.
- At the menopause, the levels of hormones produced by the ovaries decline, including estrogen and progesterone.
- Estrogen plays an important role in the anabolic process (building up complex molecules from simpler ones): it helps to regulate protein synthesis and promotes the growth and repair of tissues such as bone and muscle.
- During the menopause the anabolic effects of higher estrogen levels are lost, so it can be harder to create new muscle.

## What to do
- Anabolic resistance and declining estrogen levels can be a significant barrier to muscle growth and maintenance.
- Prioritise some strength work in your schedule.
- Do resistance exercises at least 2–3 times per week, focusing on compound exercises that work multiple muscle groups.
