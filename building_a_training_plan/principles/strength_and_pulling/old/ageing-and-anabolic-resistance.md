# Ageing and Anabolic Resistance: Why Older Climbers Need a Greater Stimulus

**Type:** Principles · **Trains:** General strength · **For:** Older

**TL;DR:** All older athletes experience anabolic resistance: muscle becomes less responsive to resistance exercise, amino acids and insulin, so building and maintaining muscle gets harder. Older climbers need more exercise, more amino acids and more insulin for the same response; prioritise some strength work, at least 2–3 resistance sessions a week built on compound exercises that work multiple muscle groups.

## Ageing: anabolic resistance
- All older athletes, not only women going through the menopause, experience anabolic resistance.
- An anabolic process is when the body builds complex molecules from simpler ones. For muscle, amino acids are formed into chains that create proteins (muscle). Anabolic processes are essential for the growth, maintenance and repair of tissues.
- Anabolic resistance: the body's tissues, particularly skeletal muscle, become less responsive to stimuli that would normally start anabolic processes, e.g. resistance exercise, the presence of amino acids (the building blocks of protein) and insulin.
- Normally the body responds to these stimuli by building up and repairing tissues, particularly muscle; with anabolic resistance the response is reduced, which makes it more difficult to build muscle and to maintain muscle mass.
- Older climbers need more exercise, more amino acids and more insulin to generate the same anabolic response as younger climbers, i.e. a greater stimulus to create the same amount of muscle.

## Why it is harder to build muscle
| Stimulus | Normal effect | With anabolic resistance |
|---|---|---|
| Resistance exercise | The muscle is exposed to mechanical stress, which triggers an anabolic response that results in muscle growth and repair | The muscles may not respond as strongly, which can make it harder to build muscle and strength |
| Amino acids | The building blocks of proteins; when consumed they can stimulate muscle protein synthesis | The muscle may not respond as effectively to them, which can limit the amount of muscle protein synthesis |
| Insulin | An anabolic stimulus that can promote the uptake of amino acids and glucose into muscle cells | The muscle may not respond as effectively to insulin, which can reduce amino-acid uptake into muscle cells and limit muscle protein synthesis |

## What to do
- Prioritise some strength work in your schedule.
- Do resistance exercises at least 2–3 times per week, focusing on compound exercises that work multiple muscle groups.
