# Mobility and Flexibility for Boulderers

**Type:** Principles · **Trains:** Mobility · **For:** Boulderers

**TL;DR:** Do not neglect mobility and flexibility: many well-conditioned climbers have almost no passive or active range of motion matching the demands of their discipline or project.

## Mobility and flexibility
- **Warning:** Do not neglect mobility and flexibility. Many well-conditioned climbers have almost no passive or active range of motion matching the demands of their discipline or project.
