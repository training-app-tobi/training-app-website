# Flexibility Without Long Static Stretching

**Type:** Principles · **Trains:** Mobility

**TL;DR:** You don't always have to do long static holds to get more flexible: instead of long static stretching holds, do mobility training, building strength through mobility.

## Flexibility without long stretching
- Instead of long static stretching holds, do mobility training (strength through mobility): you don't always have to do long static holds to get more flexible.
