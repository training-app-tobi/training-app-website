## Stretching and flexibility
- Climbers are often not very flexible. Reasons to be flexible range from injury prevention to effective performance on the wall.
- Climbers don't need extreme-flexibility-sport levels, but for very many climbers flexibility is a high-return, time-efficient, often neglected area.
- 10–30 minutes a few times a week pays huge dividends for most people.
- Flexibility training is often ignored because it doesn't give the same satisfaction as climbing or strength training: improvements take longer and are hard to measure, and people quit or take a week off and feel they lost everything. They didn't: the nervous system temporarily forgot, and it will improve.
- It is a low-risk way to improve climbing performance.
