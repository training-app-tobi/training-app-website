# Training the Whole Endurance Spectrum Against the Pump

**Type:** Principles, Planning · **Trains:** Endurance, Power endurance · **For:** Sport climbers

**TL;DR:** Don't train "at your pump limit" all year long: it is not beneficial in the long run, and getting pumped has multiple causes, so no single training method solves it. Train all 3 parts of the endurance spectrum (low-end, threshold and intervals), each at the right time of the year.

## The pump
- **Warning:** Do not train "at your pump limit" style all year long; it is not beneficial in the long run.
- Getting pumped has multiple causes, so no single training method solves it.
- Train all 3 parts of the endurance spectrum, each at the right time of the year:
  - Low-end
  - Threshold
  - Intervals
