## Who should do what
| Situation / level | Do this |
|---|---|
| Boulderers | If you have time, do some endurance training; neglecting it is one of the top mistakes of a climbing career |

## Endurance training for boulderers
- Boulderers need endurance training. If you're not strong enough there is nothing to endure, but to actually send climbs you need a large base of power endurance.
- Benefits of endurance training:
  - a useful way to backfill and finish out sessions
  - it indirectly increases work capacity
  - it implicitly improves flow and climbing quality, especially when fatigued, which improves technique if you pay attention to it
- Endurance is grossly neglected by boulderers.
- The same applies the other way round: rope climbers should do bouldering.
