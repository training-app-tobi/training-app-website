# Aerobic Capacity and Resting Time When Your Fingers Are Weak

**Type:** Principles · **Trains:** Endurance · **For:** weak fingers for your grade

**TL;DR:** If you struggle to make strength gains, a really good aerobic capacity lets you stay on the wall longer and get the most out of resting spots. Knowing how long to rest at each spot is a skill: each spot has a best resting time, after which returns diminish, so practise it in your aerobic capacity sessions.

## Aerobic capacity and resting time
- With weak fingers for your grade (fingers that may never be the strongest), find other ways to perform at your grade.
- **Aerobic capacity:** if you struggle to make strength gains, a really good aerobic capacity lets you stay on the wall longer and helps you get the most out of resting spots.
- **Optimal resting time:** not all resting positions are hands-off, and knowing how long to rest at a spot is a skill. Each spot has a best resting time, after which you get diminishing returns. Practise this in your aerobic capacity sessions; otherwise you won't get a feel for how long to rest.
