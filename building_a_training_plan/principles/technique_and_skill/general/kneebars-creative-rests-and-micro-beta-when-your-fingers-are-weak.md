## Kneebars, creative rests and micro beta
- With weak fingers for your grade (fingers that may never be the strongest), find other ways to perform at your grade.
- **Kneebars and creative rests:** kneebars can turn the crux of a route into a hands-off rest. Creative resting positions are the key to getting into harder grades, whether or not you have strong fingers or amazing endurance.
- **Micro beta:** a stronger climber may simply pull through a move, but at the limit of your ability you need to refine every single move: precise positions of feet, hands and hips, and patience in finding the sequence.
- **Rule:** if you can hold the position, you can move into the position; you just haven't found the beta yet.
