# Straight-Arm Climbing for Beginners

**Type:** Principles, Technique · **Trains:** Technique · **For:** Beginner

**TL;DR:** When you start climbing, force yourself to climb exclusively on straight arms for about the first one to two months, and keep reminding yourself to do it. Learn movement without using muscle (use your skeleton, focus and feel the movement) until you can do climbs near your limit without bending your arms.

## Who should do what
| Situation / level | Do this |
|---|---|
| Starting to climb (first 1–2 months) | Force yourself to climb exclusively on straight arms, and keep reminding yourself to do it |

## Straight-arm climbing for beginners
- When you start climbing, learn to climb on straight arms: force yourself to climb exclusively on straight arms for about the first one to two months, and keep reminding yourself to do it.
- Learn movement without using muscle: use your skeleton, focus, and feel what is going on in the movement. Have fun with it.
- Master it: be able to do climbs near your limit without bending your arms.
