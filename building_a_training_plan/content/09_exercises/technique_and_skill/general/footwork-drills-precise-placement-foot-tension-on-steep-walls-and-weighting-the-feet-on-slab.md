# Footwork Drills: Precise Placement, Foot Tension on Steep Walls and Weighting the Feet on Slab

**Type:** Exercise/protocol, Technique · **Trains:** Technique

**TL;DR:** Spend about 10 minutes of the warm-up placing your feet precisely, silently and slowly on easy boulder problems. On a steeper wall, traverse the feet with static hands and cut and re-place the feet on an easy steep boulder; on slab, climb one-handed or no-handed for around 10–20 minutes to weight your feet.

## Drills
| Drill | How | Dose |
|---|---|---|
| Precise footwork (warm-up) | Find an area of wall with a selection of easy boulder problems; the focus is precision, not sending. Keep eye contact with the foothold and your foot all the way through, until the foot is placed exactly how you want it. Place your feet silently and slowly. Look for pressure through your feet: keep the upper body relaxed and don't grip any handholds too hard | About 10 minutes, as part of the warm-up |
| Hands static, feet traverse (steeper wall) | Keep the handholds static and traverse your feet around onto different footholds, exploring different foot positions and the toeing-in motion on footholds | Around 6–10 foot movements, 4 times on different areas of the wall |
| Cut and re-place the feet (steeper wall) | Choose a steep boulder problem that is relatively easy for you; on each move, purposefully cut your feet from the wall, then bring them back in and place them precisely on the footholds | Around 3–4 repetitions of the problem |
| One-handed or no-handed slab climbing | Use an easier-angled wall; it doesn't have to be on set boulders (use any colour), which is a chance to get creative with where you send your feet | Around 10–20 minutes |

- **Warning:** the two steeper-wall drills are on steeper areas of wall, so be well warmed up before doing them.
- Slab drill: weighting the feet often depends a lot on hip position. When working out the moves, think about where your hips are relative to your feet; this is a really good cue.
- Not using your arms doesn't mean you don't need to rest.
