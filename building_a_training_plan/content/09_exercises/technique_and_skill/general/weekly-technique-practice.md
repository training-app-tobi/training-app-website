# Weekly Technique Practice: Ten Attempts on Four Technical Boulders

**Type:** Exercise/protocol, Technique · **Trains:** Technique

**TL;DR:** Once a week, pick 4 boulders whose difficulty is nearly all in the technique, below 80% of your physical maximum, and give each 10 attempts with full recovery in between: the same beta every time, each attempt cleaner and more efficient than the last. It also works on a 2–4-move section of a highly technical boulder at or above your limit. Choose movements you have less practice with, based on your weaknesses.

## Weekly technique practice
- A drill that uses repetition to build technique: repeat the same climbs, with the same beta, until you climb them as well as you can.

| Part | Details |
|---|---|
| Intention | Develop confidence and proper movement mechanics for movements you have less practice with |
| Focus | Use the same beta on every attempt; execute perfect technique and repeat the bodily sensations that lead to success (tightness in certain areas, shifting of weight); place your feet in the perfect spot on every foothold; root down through the anterior and posterior core; climb fluently, without hesitation |
| Problem choice | Below 80% of your physical intensity maximum, with nearly all of its difficulty in the technical nature of its movements |
| Attempts | 10 attempts on each boulder, resting enough between attempts to be fully recovered each time; make every attempt better by executing the beta as flawlessly and efficiently as possible |
| Volume | 4 different boulder problems |
| Difficulty | Ideally hard enough to allow 3–6 good laps |
| Best done on | A boulder at or even above your limit, repeating a section of 2–4 moves; the moves should be highly technical and less dependent on physical strength |

- The aim is the most ideal stimulus for your own skill development, based on your weaknesses.
