# Strength-Block Bouldering: Strength Building Blocks and 3x3 Hard Boulders

**Type:** Exercise/protocol · **Trains:** Finger strength, General strength, Power

**TL;DR:** In a strength block (not a power block), keep only one or two dynamic limit boulders to maintain some power, and use high-intensity drills that load you on the wall without moving very powerfully. Basic strength: about 12–22 strength building blocks at a lower RPE and higher volume, replacing climbs with harder ones when they drop to about RPE 5 so the RPE comes back to 6.5–7. Max strength: 3x3, your hardest, not very dynamic boulder of 4–8 moves (about 30 s under tension), 3 times with about 5 min rest, repeated, varying the boulders.

## Bouldering in a strength block
- In a strength focus block (not power), dynamic limit boulders aren't the go-to; keep one or two to maintain some power.
- For max strength, use drills that give high-intensity loading on the wall without moving very powerfully.

| Strength block | Intensity and volume | Drill |
|---|---|---|
| Basic strength | Lower the RPE and increase the volume (number of climbs or attempts) | Strength building blocks: about 12–22 in a build-up; when the RPE of each climb drops to about 5, replace those climbs with harder ones to bring the RPE back up to 6.5–7 |
| Max strength | Increase the intensity and decrease the volume | 3x3: a really hard boulder, the hardest thing you can do, not very dynamic (maybe one dynamic move), ideally 4–8 moves, about 30 s time under tension. Do it 3 times with about 5 min rest in between, repeat, and vary the boulders |
