# Sub-Limit Bouldering: Boulders That Go in One to Four Attempts

**Type:** Exercise/protocol, Session · **Trains:** Power, Technique

**TL;DR:** Work four to eight boulders per session on a system board, each one that takes one to four attempts: that is the sweet spot where true limit movement meets successful execution. Prioritize the highest possible movement quality, rest properly between attempts, and stop before any quality dips. Use a wide variety of grip types, movement styles and wall angles.

## What the drill is for
- Sub-limit bouldering is designed to sharpen your max-level climbing skill set while experiencing a diverse range of grip types and movement styles.
- Prioritize the highest possible movement quality and maintain a high level of output during the entire session.
- Physical focus: fully committing to every move, building explosiveness, tension and full-body awareness.
- **Warning:** Respect your peak power state by stopping before any quality dips in the session.

## Protocol
| Element | Prescription |
|---|---|
| Boulder difficulty | Boulders that take one to four attempts: the sweet spot where true limit movement meets successful execution |
| Boulders per session | Four to eight, on a system board |
| Boulder choice | A wide variety of movement styles with a lot of different grip types |
| Wall angles | Different wall angles during the session, from 70° overhung, maybe up to 25° overhung, and everything in between |
| Rest between attempts | Proper rest, essential for full recovery and recharge |

## How to approach each attempt
- Be extremely intentional with your beta: refine movement and make small tweaks for improvement fast, because you only have four attempts.
- Each attempt should be purposeful, with a clear goal.
- The aim is to build adaptability and technical robustness.
- Treat every fall as data for improvement, not as a setback or an indication of failure. This mindset fosters calmness, resilience and tactical flexibility, which are crucial for hard redpointing and competitions.
