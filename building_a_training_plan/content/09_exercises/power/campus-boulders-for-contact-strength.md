# Campus Boulders for Contact Strength

**Type:** Exercise/protocol · **Trains:** Power, Finger strength

**TL;DR:** Campus boulders (climb boulders without your feet) to train contact strength. Progress through three boulders: a very easy one on jugs with full control and maximal speed, a step-up that takes about 1 to 2 tries at full effort, then maximal-difficulty boulders that take up to 2 to 4 sessions, like limit bouldering without your feet.

## The three boulders

| Boulder | Difficulty | Aim |
|---|---|---|
| 1. First boulder | Very easy, on jugs | Full control and maximal speed |
| 2. Second boulder | A step up: something that takes about 1 to 2 tries; the holds can get a little worse | Go to full effort, pull harder and pull a little faster; get ready for the next level |
| 3. Third level: maximal-difficulty boulders | Should take up to 2 to 4 sessions to do; like limit bouldering, but without your feet | Stretch your limits in how hard the holds you can hit are, and exert contact strength: maximum force, applied as fast as needed to stick the moves |

## First boulder: focus
- Own every hold, close the hand as fast as possible, and pull as fast as possible.
- Coordinate the body: keep the right muscles tight and loosen them at the right time, to create flow and allow better speed through the moves.

## Form and preparation
- Proper form: keep the shoulders engaged and be strong in the arms.
- Be fully rested, so you can be powerful in the arms and the fingers.
- Be fully warm and prepared, and get as psyched as possible before every single attempt.
- Get on a wider variety of holds: don't hit the same grip type over and over.
