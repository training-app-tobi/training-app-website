# Maximum Pulling Strength: Weighted Pull-ups, One-Arm Lock-offs and One-Arm Pull-ups

**Type:** Exercise/protocol · **Trains:** Pulling strength · **For:** Intermediate, Advanced, Elite

**TL;DR:** Train maximum pulling strength with brief, near-maximal efforts: 3 to 5 reps or 90% of 1RM, with at least 3 minutes of rest so every set is all-out, 3 sets for intermediate, 4 for advanced and 5 for elite climbers. Use weighted (hypergravity) pull-ups with enough weight to make 5 reps difficult, one-arm lock-offs held for 5 seconds, and one-arm (or one-arm-assisted) pull-ups for 1 to 5 reps.

## How to train maximum strength
- **Warning:** For beginners, highly targeted strength training could lead to injury.

| Element | Prescription |
|---|---|
| Effort | Brief exercises of 3 to 5 reps, or 90% of 1RM |
| Rest between sets | Near-complete recovery, at least 3 minutes, so that every set is an all-out effort |
| Sets, intermediate | 3 |
| Sets, advanced | 4 |
| Sets, elite | 5 |

### Climbing-specific strength exercises
| Exercise | Load / duration |
|---|---|
| Weighted (hypergravity) pull-ups | Enough added weight to make 5 reps difficult |
| One-arm lock-offs | Held for 5 seconds |
| One-arm pull-ups (or one-arm-assisted pull-ups) | 1 to 5 reps |
