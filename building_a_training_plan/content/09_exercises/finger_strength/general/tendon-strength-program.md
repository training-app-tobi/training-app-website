# Tendon Strength Program: Open-Hand Max Hangs and Weighted Pull-ups

**Type:** Exercise/protocol · **Trains:** Finger strength, Pulling strength · **For:** Advanced, Older

**TL;DR:** A 9-week program for stiffer, stronger finger tendons: maximal open-hand hangs on an edge of around 20 mm, 2–3 sessions a week, building from 3 sets of 20 s to 5 sets of 5–10 s, plus 5 sets of 5 weighted pull-ups twice a week from week 4, then a deload in week 9. Keep every hang and pull-up at maximal effort without chasing quick progression; do the hangs before climbing and the pull-ups on non-climbing days.

## Who should do what
| Situation / level | Do this |
|---|---|
| Already used to a high training load on the fingerboard | Skip the first 2 weeks of the program. |

## Program
- **Warning:** The program gives the body time to adapt to a higher workload. Once used to this high workload you should not fear injury more than usual; the process of getting there is what can be dangerous. Many injuries happen when the load is ramped up too quickly.
- Hangs: open hand, on an edge of around 20 mm, always warmed up and from a rested state, and only once you already know your way around the fingerboard. Adjust the weight so the hang feels maximal but you could hold on for a couple more seconds than required.
- Add weight so hangs and pull-ups stay maximal effort. Don't worry about quick progression.

| Week | Sessions per week | Fingerboard | Pull-ups |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | 2x fingerboard | 3 sets: hang 20 s, rest 3 min | — |
| 2 | 2x fingerboard | 5 sets: hang 20 s, rest 3 min | — |
| 3 | 3x fingerboard | 5 sets: hang 20 s, rest 3 min | — |
| 4 | 3x fingerboard, 2x pull-ups | 5 sets: hang 20 s, rest 3 min | 5 sets of 5 reps, rest 3-5 min |
| 5-8 | 3x fingerboard, 2x pull-ups | 5 sets: hang 5-10 s, rest 3-5 min | 5 sets of 5 reps, rest 3-5 min |
| 9 | Deload | — | — |

- Before the deload, ramp the training load up a little more (functionally overreach) so the supercompensation is more effective. After the week-9 deload, repeat weeks 5-8 as needed.
- Fitting it around climbing: do the hangs before climbing and the pull-ups on non-climbing days.
- The program also gives neuromuscular gains and explosiveness, not only tendon strength.
