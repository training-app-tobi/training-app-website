# Finger and Climbing-Specific Strength Across Five Qualities

**Type:** Exercise/protocol · **Trains:** Finger strength, Power

**TL;DR:** Apply the five strength qualities to finger and climbing training: unilateral single-arm hangs on a 20 mm edge (6+6 s), bilateral 2-arm pocket hangs (6 s), explosive campus ladders (4+4), isometric 1-arm holds on a pull-up bar (6 s + 6 s) and integrative power boulder problems at onsight level (12 s max). This version favours isometrics; cover the easy ground first and get into the details once everything else is dialled.

## Finger and climbing-specific version

- The five strength qualities are unilateral, bilateral, explosive, isometric and integrative (defined in the note General Strength Sessions: Movement Patterns x Strength Qualities).

The same five qualities applied to climbing or finger training:

| Quality | Exercise | Dose |
|---|---|---|
| Unilateral | Single-arm edge hang, 20 mm | 6+6 sec |
| Bilateral | 2-arm hang, pocket, first pair | 6 sec |
| Explosive | Campus ladder, singles, small rung | 4+4 |
| Isometric | 1-arm iso hold on pull-up bar | 6 sec + 6 sec |
| Integrative | Power boulder problem at onsight level | 12 sec max |

- This version favours isometrics. You don't need to stand at the hangboard for an hour doing the same sets and reps for everything and micro-managing load: that is time-consuming and maybe not that useful.
- Find where you are lacking most and where you can make big gains. Cover the easy ground first, then get into the details once everything else is dialled.
