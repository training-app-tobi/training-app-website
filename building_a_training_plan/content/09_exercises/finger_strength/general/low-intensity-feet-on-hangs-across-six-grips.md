# Low-Intensity Feet-On Hangs Across Six Grips

**Type:** Exercise/protocol · **Trains:** Finger strength, Recovery

**TL;DR:** A 10-minute low-intensity hangboard workout that works through a range of half crimp and open-hand grip positions, six hangs on a 20 mm edge. Keep the intensity very low throughout: your feet never leave the ground. It is built to be repeated frequently, to support tendon adaptation and long-term finger health.

## The workout
- A 10-minute low-intensity hangboard workout that works through a range of half crimp and open-hand grip positions.
- Keep the intensity very low throughout: your feet never leave the ground. Lower onto your fingers and apply load while keeping the rate of effort minimal.
- **Warning:** The workout is built to be repeated frequently; doing it at high intensity leads to overtraining and injury.

## Exercises
| Order | Grip | Edge |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Half 4: four-finger half crimp | 20 mm |
| 2 | F3 Open: front three open | 20 mm |
| 3 | M2 Open: middle two open | 20 mm |
| 4 | F2 Open: front two open | 20 mm |
| 5 | B3 Half: back three half crimp | 20 mm |
| 6 | F3 Half: front three half crimp | 20 mm |

- Every hang: proper shoulder engagement and a slight bend in the elbows.
- Keep the finger position consistent throughout each hang; if the fingers begin to open or shift, the load may need adjusting.
- Use a pulley system or added weight to reach the correct intensity.

## Why it works
- Stimulates low-level force production in the finger flexors and the connective tissues of the hand without accumulating fatigue, which supports tendon adaptation and long-term finger health when done consistently over time.
