# A 12-Week Progressive Overload Protocol for Finger Strength

**Type:** Exercise/protocol · **Trains:** Finger strength

**TL;DR:** Test your finger strength in your chosen grip and hang time, then train for 12 weeks in three mesocycles of 3 weeks on and 1 week off, starting at 85% of the tested max. In the on weeks one session raises the load in 1–3% steps once all reps are clean, and the other stays at the same % of max; the off weeks keep only the progressively loaded session. Retest after 12 weeks exactly as the first time.

## The protocol
- An example of progressive overload for the fingers: add load over the weeks by changing intensity, volume or frequency, one at a time and only in small steps.
- Choose the grip position and time under tension first (the note Finger Strength Training: Methods, Grip Types and Progressive Overload explains how), then keep them for the whole protocol.

| Step | What to do |
|---|---|
| Start | Test your finger strength in your preferred grip position for your preferred time under tension |
| Weeks 1–12 | Slowly increase the load from 85% of maximum, making only small increases in intensity, volume or frequency |
| Structure | Mesocycles of 3 weeks on, 1 week off: 3 mesocycles in 12 weeks |
| "On" weeks, session 1 (progressively loaded) | Increase the load in 1–3% increments; complete all reps with good form before increasing |
| "On" weeks, session 2 (constant loaded) | Keep the same % of max intensity or volume for the whole 12 weeks |
| "Off" weeks | 1 fingerboard session only: the progressively loaded session at the 1–3% load you did the previous week |
| After 12 weeks | Retest exactly as in the first step |

- This protocol is a general suggestion: modify it to suit individual needs; if unsure, speak to a coach.
- **Warning:** Don't start a finger training session at your working load: build up to it gradually over 3–4 sets, in every session.
