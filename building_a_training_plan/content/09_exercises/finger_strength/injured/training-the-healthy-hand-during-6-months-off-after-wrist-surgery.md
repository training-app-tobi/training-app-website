# Training the Healthy Hand During 6 Months Off After Wrist Surgery

**Type:** Exercise/protocol, Injury & health · **Trains:** Finger strength · **For:** injured or returning

Aim for very slow, steady improvement and don't try to max out its strength, or you end up with an imbalance between the hands; alternating lighter repeaters with max hangs is another option.

## Training the healthy hand during 6 months off after wrist surgery
- Unable to climb for 6 months after wrist surgery: on the healthy hand do basic density hangs or repeaters to build resilience. Not super intense: build capacity and keep that hand healthy.
- Work on the operated wrist 100%.
- **Warning:** Don't overdo it on the healthy hand: aim for very slow, steady improvement. Don't try to max out its strength: over 6 months you would end up with an imbalance between the hands.
- Any of the protocols can work: repeaters can improve max strength; max hangs, if they are really long, can improve local endurance. What a hang trains depends on the protocol used, not on the tool.
- Suggested: density hangs, maybe with some recruitment pulls mixed in to keep what you have.
- A blend that is typically given to people who have both hands but can't use their feet: alternate sessions.

| Session | Do this |
|---|---|
| One session | Lighter repeaters, to stay metabolic |
| Next session | Max hangs |
