# A Little Antagonist Training in Every Session

**Type:** Exercise/protocol, Planning · **Trains:** Antagonists & core

**TL;DR:** Antagonist training belongs in every climber's programme, year round: neglecting it can lead to imbalances, injury and time off. Include a little in every training and climbing session: full range of motion exercises with a resistance band at low intensity in the warm-up, and 5 to 8 reps of shoulder press, bench press or push-ups at the end of a climbing workout.

## Antagonist training
- Antagonist training should be part of every climber's programme, and year round. Neglecting it can lead to imbalances, which can result in injury, and an injured climber can't train.
- Include a little antagonist training in every training and climbing session.

| When | What | Dose |
|---|---|---|
| Warm-up | Full range of motion (ROM) exercises with a resistance band | Low intensity |
| End of a climbing workout | Shoulder press, bench press or push-ups | 5 to 8 reps |
