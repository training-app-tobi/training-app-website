# A Simple Core Session: Twisting Crunches, Dish Tucks and Supermans

**Type:** Exercise/protocol · **Trains:** Antagonists & core

**TL;DR:** Three floor exercises, 3 sets of 15 reps each with 1 minute rest: twisting crunches, dish tucks and supermans. Start at moderate intensity, completing all sets comfortably with excellent form, and push harder only once you are conditioned to the exercises. Don't let the lower back arch, and stop for the day if form or session quality slips.

## Core session
3 floor exercises, 3 sets each.

| Exercise | Sets | Reps per set | Rest |
|---|---|---|---|
| Twisting crunch | 3 | 15 | 1 min |
| Dish tuck | 3 | 15 | 1 min |
| Superman | 3 | 15 | 1 min |

| Stage | Intensity |
|---|---|
| Start | Moderate intensity: comfortably complete all sets with excellent form, not approaching exhaustion. Aim first for the best form and a higher total volume, so you become conditioned to the exercises |
| Once conditioned to the exercises | Push the intensity harder |

- If you are struggling, ask a friend for support/assistance.
- **Warning:** Don't let the lower back arch.
- **Warning:** As in all conditioning sessions, form must be excellent throughout and session quality high. If either is lost, stop for the day and adjust the next session.
