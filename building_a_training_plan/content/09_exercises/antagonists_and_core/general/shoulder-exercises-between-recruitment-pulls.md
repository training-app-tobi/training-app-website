# Shoulder Exercises Between Recruitment Pulls: No Moneys and Stick Dislocates

**Type:** Exercise/protocol · **Trains:** Antagonists & core, Mobility

**TL;DR:** Use the rests between working sets of recruitment pulls for shoulder work that strengthens the shoulders, improves climbing performance and reduces the chance of injury: "no moneys" with a resistance band for the external rotators (8–12 reps, up to 3 sets) and dynamic stick dislocates for gentle shoulder mobility (30–60 seconds, up to 3 sets).

## Before climbing: shoulder work during the rests
- Use the rests between working sets of recruitment pulls for shoulder work. It is more than a warm-up: an introductory way to strengthen the shoulders, improving climbing performance and reducing the chance of injury on the wall.

| Exercise | Purpose | How | Dose |
|---|---|---|---|
| "No moneys" with a resistance band | External rotators of the rotator cuff | Start with the hands close together, pull the hands apart, squeezing the shoulder blades back and down. Control the motion the whole time and don't let the band go slack | 8–12 reps per set, up to 3 sets |
| Stick dislocates | Gentle shoulder mobility | Keep the motion dynamic. If they are new to you or you have no stick, use a band | 30–60 seconds, up to 3 sets |
