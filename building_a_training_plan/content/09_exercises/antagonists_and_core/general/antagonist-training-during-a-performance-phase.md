# Antagonist Training During a Performance Phase

**Type:** Exercise/protocol, Planning · **Trains:** Antagonists & core · **For:** performance phase

**TL;DR:** Doing just a little antagonist training during a performance phase can improve the whole climbing year. Two short sessions per week, after climbing days, at moderate intensity: one exercise each for upper traps, triceps and chest, rotator cuff and forearm extensors, 3 sets of 8-12 reps with a few minutes' rest between sets.

## Why do it during a performance phase
- A performance phase means working towards a specific goal, or climbing as much as possible while the weather is good. Doing just a little antagonist training during this time can improve the whole climbing year.
- When climbing a lot with reduced supplementary conditioning, the body becomes more specialised. This is good for a short period because it gives the best results, but it can backfire if it goes on too long.
- Adding moderate-intensity exercises for the muscles used less avoids an increasing imbalance in strength. This helps sustain a longer peak performance and makes injury less likely when returning to training later in the year.

## Protocol
| Element | Prescription |
|---|---|
| Frequency | Two short sessions per week: enough to maintain a healthy strength balance for most climbers, and very unlikely to cause fatigue that would hurt climbing |
| Timing | After climbing days, so that training is not added to rest days |
| Intensity | Moderate intensity is all that is needed |
| Sets and reps | 3 sets of 8-12 reps |
| Rest | A few minutes between sets |

## Exercises
Do one exercise for each area.

| Area | Exercise options |
|---|---|
| Upper traps | Shoulder shrugs or shoulder press |
| Triceps and chest | Tricep dips, press-ups or bench press |
| Rotator cuff | Standing IYT or external rotations |
| Forearm extensors | Forearm curls |

## Muscle mass
- This volume is usually used for hypertrophy, but because the intensity is moderate and the frequency is much lower than climbing, it is not a concern for anyone worried about gaining muscle mass.
