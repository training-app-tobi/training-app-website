# Shoulder and Core Conditioning Before a Bouldering Trip

**Type:** Exercise/protocol, Planning · **Trains:** Antagonists & core · **For:** preparing a bouldering trip

**TL;DR:** Prone shoulder rotations and reverse flyes build shoulder stability, helping you pull the chest close to the wall, stabilise movements and get around weak small-hold performance. Add a core session only in the middle mesocycle for a climber who does little core training and already has a gym session and a lot of board climbing: it raises the load mid-plan and adds body tension.

## Specific conditioning
- From a 12-week plan for a V9 boulderer's summer bouldering trip: the climbing sessions are planned first, then the fingerboard sessions, the weights-gym session and specific conditioning.

| Exercise | What it does |
|---|---|
| Prone shoulder rotations | Shoulder stability; helps you pull the chest close to the wall and stabilise movements |
| Reverse flyes | Shoulder stability; helps you pull the chest close to the wall and stabilise movements |

- The shoulder exercises also help performance on small holds, a way around weak small-hold performance.
- Core: a core session only in the middle mesocycle, for a climber who does little core training and already has a gym session and a lot of board climbing: it raises the load in the middle mesocycle and keeps the start and finish manageable; it adds body tension.
