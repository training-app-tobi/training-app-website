# 6 Boulders in 6 Minutes

**Type:** Exercise/protocol · **Trains:** Power endurance

**TL;DR:** Climb six rehearsed boulder problems on the minute, one attempt per problem, across multiple sets in a 28-minute session. The compressed timeframe and short rest drive rapid fatigue, so you must perform under rapidly accumulating fatigue. Choose problems you can reliably complete when fresh: the challenge comes from the short rest, not from the difficulty of the problems.

## Session

| Element | Detail |
|---|---|
| Session length | 28 min |
| Format | Six boulder problems on the minute: one problem started every minute, six problems in six minutes |
| Attempts | One attempt allowed per problem |
| Sets | Multiple sets |

## Intent
- Develop repeated anaerobic output, with each boulder completed on the minute.
- The compressed timeframe and short rest drive rapid fatigue, so you must perform under rapidly accumulating fatigue.

## Choosing problems
- Use six rehearsed problems that you can reliably complete when fresh; the challenge comes from the short rest, not from the difficulty of the problems.
