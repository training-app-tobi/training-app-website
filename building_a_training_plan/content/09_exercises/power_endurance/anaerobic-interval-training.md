# Anaerobic Interval Training: 1:4 Intervals, 4×4s and Pyramids

**Type:** Exercise/protocol · **Trains:** Power endurance

**TL;DR:** Anaerobic training builds muscular endurance by working close to the pump: hard, pumpy but brief work, then rest that is not complete recovery, on a strict time schedule. Use 1:4 intervals on a treadwall or systems board (1 minute all out, 4 minutes rest, 6 to 8 cycles), 4×4s on boulders two to three grades below your redpoint level, or a pyramid of 1 to 4 minutes on and off and back down, with first-timers doing just the first three steps.

## Anaerobic training

- Anaerobic training means working close to the pump: work hard, rest well but not to complete recovery, then repeat. The work is pumpy and taxing but brief, just long enough that you brush up against your limits.
- Use intervals of hard work followed by an equal or slightly longer period of rest, on a strict time schedule. The timing helps you find the intensity that lasts exactly the time on the clock (blasted by the pump but not losing your grip) and gives just enough rest to go again while still tired from the last round.

| Workout | How |
|---|---|
| 1:4 intervals (treadwall or systems board) | 1 minute all out, to the point of almost falling when the time is up; rest 4 minutes; 6 to 8 cycles. |
| 4×4 | Pick four boulders about two to three grades below your redpoint level. Climb the first boulder four times in a row with no rest (back on the wall as soon as your feet touch the ground), then rest 3 minutes. Repeat with the second, third and fourth boulder, 3 minutes' rest after each. Right difficulty: on the verge of falling (but not quite) on the third and fourth reps of the last two boulders. |
| Pyramid | 1 minute climbing / 1 minute rest, then 2 on / 2 off, 3 on / 3 off, 4 on / 4 off, then back down to 1. |

### Pyramid scaling

| Level | Pyramid |
|---|---|
| First-timers | Start with just the first three steps |
| More advanced climbers | Add a fifth step (5 on / 5 off) at the top |
