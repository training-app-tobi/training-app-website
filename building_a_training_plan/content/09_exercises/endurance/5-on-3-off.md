# 5 On 3 Off

**Type:** Exercise/protocol · **Trains:** Endurance, Recovery

**TL;DR:** Climb continuously for 5 minutes at very low intensity, then rest for 3 minutes, and repeat for multiple sets on routes or circuits, 21 min in total. The light climbing intervals promote recovery by increasing blood flow to aid tissue repair, while building base endurance. Keep the intensity very low throughout.

## When to use it
- As an active recovery exercise.
- As a shorter interval format to build up towards longer ARC durations.

## Protocol
| Step | What to do | Time |
|---|---|---|
| Work | Climb continuously on routes or circuits at very low intensity | 5 min |
| Rest | Rest | 3 min |
| Repeat | Multiple sets; keep the intensity very low throughout | 21 min total |

## Why it works
- Light climbing intervals increase blood flow to aid tissue repair, which promotes recovery while building base endurance.
