# Training Climbing Endurance: Aerobic Capacity and Regeneration

**Type:** Exercise/protocol · **Trains:** Endurance, Recovery · **For:** Route climbers, Boulderers

**TL;DR:** Three endurance sessions. Aerobic capacity: 10 × 1-minute intervals at 2 grades below your onsight with 1 minute rest, pumped only at the end of the session. Regeneration (Continuity 5x3): 3 × 5 minutes at least 4 grades below your onsight with 3 minutes rest, so easy you could chat while climbing. ARC: 20–45 minutes of uninterrupted climbing well below your onsight. Vary the terrain and hold sizes for aerobic capacity, and keep regeneration at zero discomfort.

## Sessions
- What endurance is, why both boulderers and route climbers need it and how long it takes are in the note Climbing Endurance: Aerobic Capacity, Regeneration and How Long It Takes. How many endurance sessions to do each week is in the note Endurance Sessions per Week for Route Climbers and Boulderers.

| Session | Sets × reps | Work | Rest between reps | Grade | Pump level | Where |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Aerobic capacity | 1 × 10 | 1 minute of climbing | 1 minute | 2 grades below your onsight | High only at the end of the session | Bouldering walls, top-ropes, lead |
| Regeneration: Continuity 5x3 | 3 × 1 | 5 minutes of climbing | 3 minutes | At least 4 grades below your onsight | Extremely low or non-existent | Bouldering wall, roped wall or a very easy foot-on campus board |
| ARC (Aerobic Restoration and Capillarity) | 1 continuous effort | 20–45 minutes of uninterrupted climbing | None | Well below onsight level | So low you barely feel pumped | — |

### Aerobic capacity
- The grade must be below your onsight: this gives a high chance that the intensity is low enough to be an aerobic stimulus and that you won't train anaerobically.
- Choose varied terrain and don't always use the same hold size.

### Regeneration
- Purpose: assist physical recovery from more intense training and improve localised blood flow. If the intensity is right, it can be done on rest days or at the end of hard, exhaustive training sessions.
- The grade must be a long way below your onsight. Play it safer than you think: the session must feel extremely easy, so easy that you could switch off from the climbing and chat to someone while doing it.
- Zero discomfort is perfect.
- "4 grades below" means letter grades.

### ARC
- ARC is continuous low-intensity climbing, essentially an extended version of regeneration work. It builds aerobic base and improves blood flow to the forearms, and overlaps closely with regeneration training.
