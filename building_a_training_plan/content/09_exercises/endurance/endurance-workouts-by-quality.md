# Endurance Workouts by Quality, With Two 8-Session Progressions

**Type:** Exercise/protocol · **Trains:** Endurance, Power endurance, Power

**TL;DR:** One workout for each of the four endurance qualities. Aerobic capacity: more and more easy pitches or laps at a fixed difficulty. Aerobic power: really intense sets such as 4 boulder problems back-to-back with long rests, 2–3 times a week for 3 weeks. Anaerobic capacity: a circuit of very short exercises (12 s or less) with 20–30 s rests, advanced by adding sets and rounds. Anaerobic power: 5–15 s efforts at your limit with equal rest for 4–5 sets, then 8–10 min rest. Two 8-session progressions: the anaerobic capacity circuit (1–2 times a week) and route 4x4s for aerobic capacity (2–3 times a week).

## Workouts by quality

- When a home wall or gym lacks some of the tools, build your own sessions by keeping the **duration** and **difficulty** of the exercises in mind; with some creativity almost any facet of fitness can be trained in almost any facility.

| Quality | What it is | Workout | Dose | How to advance |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Aerobic capacity | "Extensive endurance": easy, continuous climbing | Mostly built by climbing routes; long traverses or treadwall laps work for some, but most people benefit from simply doing pitches and aiming to do more of them. Toproping with a partner (I-go-you-go) is fun and effective | More climbing each workout for several weeks | Do more work at a fixed difficulty. Eventually more pitches aren't logistically possible and other methods are needed |
| Aerobic power | What most mean by "power endurance": sustaining relatively powerful efforts under fatigue | Really intense sets, e.g. 4 boulder problems back-to-back, with long rests so the next efforts can be done at the same intensity | Maybe 2–3 times per week for 3 weeks, then switch to less intense endurance workouts | Get better at managing these efforts over the 3–4 weeks, not totally fatigued on day one |
| Anaerobic capacity | Most climbers never train in this zone, and it can be really good for them | Very short sets (12 s or less), with rests that let you work well on the next exercise; several exercises in a series (example circuit below) | Circuit with 20–30 s rest after each exercise; rest 3 min and repeat | Add sets and rounds to the circuit, not make the exercises harder (adding weight or shortening rests) |
| Anaerobic power | Very hard; requires discipline | Short boulders of 2–5 moves, or break a circuit into sections as on a project | Work 5–15 s, rest the same amount of time, repeat for 4–5 sets: 1–2 min at the limit of what you can climb. Then rest a long time (8–10 min) and do it again | — |

- **Warning:** aerobic power workouts are hard and feel like endurance training straight away, but they are very taxing to sustain over long periods and interfere with other training adaptations.

Anaerobic capacity example circuit:

| Order | Exercise | Rest after |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | 3 pull-ups | 20–30 s |
| 2 | 10 s edge hang | 20–30 s |
| 3 | 5 box jumps | 20–30 s |
| 4 | 5 inverted rows | 20–30 s |
| 5 | 10 s edge hang | 20–30 s |
| Then | Rest 3 min and repeat | — |

## Two 8-session progressions

- Each progression makes the workout a bit harder every session, so the endurance work runs long enough to change your body.

Anaerobic capacity, 8-session progression, 1–2 times per week. A "series" is a group of 4–6 exercises.

| Session | Series | Sets | Exercises | Rest between exercises | Rest between sets | Rest between series |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | 1 | 2 | 5 | 0:40 | 5:00 | n/a |
| 2 | 1 | 3 | 5 | 0:40 | 5:00 | n/a |
| 3 | 1 | 4 | 5 | 0:40 | 5:00 | n/a |
| 4 | 1 | 4 | 6 | 0:40 | 5:00 | n/a |
| 5 | 2 | 3 | 5 | 0:40 | 5:00 | 10:00 |
| 6 | 2 | 4 | 5 | 0:40 | 5:00 | 10:00 |
| 7 | 2 | 4 | 6 | 0:40 | 5:00 | 10:00 |
| 8 | 2 | 5 | 5 | 0:40 | 5:00 | 10:00 |

Aerobic capacity, 8-session progression, route 4x4s, 2–3 times per week.

| Session | Series | Routes per series | Rest between pitches | Route difficulty | Rest between series |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | 3 | 3 | As needed | Easiest available | 1:1 or about 8 min |
| 2 | 4 | 3 | As needed | At least 5 grades below flash grade | 1:1 or about 8 min |
| 3 | 4 | 4 | Lower off only | At least 5 grades below flash grade | 1:1 or about 8 min |
| 4–8 | 4 | 4 | Lower off only | Increase one series by one grade if all routes were completed last time | 1:1 or about 8 min |
