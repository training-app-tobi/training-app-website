# 50:50 Easy to Moderate

**Type:** Exercise/protocol · **Trains:** Endurance, Technique

**TL;DR:** In each rep, alternate between 15 easy moves and 15 moderate moves, i.e. easier and harder sections of climbing within the same rep, 43 min in total. The purpose is to develop aerobic capacity while learning to adapt pacing and climbing style between sections of different intensity. Your pump should ebb and flow with the changing difficulty and never reach the point of failure.

## Protocol
| Step | What to do | Amount |
|---|---|---|
| Easy section | Climb easy moves | 15 moves |
| Moderate section | Climb moderate moves | 15 moves |
| Each rep | Alternate between the easy and moderate sections within the same rep | 43 min total |

- **Warning:** Your pump should ebb and flow with the changing difficulty, and never reach the point of failure.

## Why it works
- Develops aerobic capacity while you learn to adapt pacing and climbing style between sections of different intensity, i.e. practising the skill of switching (adjusting) intensity mid-climb.
