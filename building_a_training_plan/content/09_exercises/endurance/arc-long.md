# ARC Long

**Type:** Exercise/protocol · **Trains:** Endurance, Recovery

**TL;DR:** Climb continuously for 30 minutes at very low intensity, keeping movement easy on routes or circuits and never approaching failure. The extended block of easy climbing promotes active recovery and builds an aerobic base, supporting long-term endurance development. The long time on the wall maximises capillarisation and aerobic adaptation in the forearms.

## Session
| What to do | Time | Load score |
|---|---|---|
| Climb continuously at very low intensity, keeping movement easy on routes or circuits and never approaching failure | 30 min | 45 |

- Intent: active recovery and building an aerobic base through an extended block of easy climbing, supporting long-term endurance development.
- **Warning:** The long duration requires sustained focus on keeping intensity appropriately low.

## Why it works
- The longer time on the wall maximises capillarisation and aerobic adaptation in the forearms.
