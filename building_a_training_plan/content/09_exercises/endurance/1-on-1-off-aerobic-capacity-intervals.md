# 1 On 1 Off Aerobic Capacity Intervals

**Type:** Exercise/protocol · **Trains:** Endurance

**TL;DR:** Structured mid-intensity intervals with a 1:1 work-to-rest ratio: climb for 1 minute at a moderate intensity, rest for 1 minute, and repeat for multiple sets, 19 min in total. The intervals stress the aerobic capacity system and become progressively harder to recover from by the end of the workout. Develop a manageable pump during each climbing interval, but always recover enough to keep quality in the next set.

## Protocol
| Step | What to do | Time |
|---|---|---|
| Work | Climb a route or circuit at a moderate intensity; develop a manageable pump | 1 min |
| Rest | Step off the wall and rest; recover enough to keep quality in the next set | 1 min |
| Repeat | Multiple sets | 19 min total |
