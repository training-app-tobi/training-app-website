# Stretching for Growing Youth Climbers

**Type:** Exercise/protocol · **Trains:** Mobility · **For:** Youth

**TL;DR:** Most climbing moves don't need extraordinary flexibility, but fast-growing bones and muscles can cause a growing sense of tightness that limits movement, so a moderate amount of daily stretching helps youth climbers keep moving smoothly: gentle stretching of the arms, torso and legs in the warm-up before maximal climbing, and 10–15 minutes each evening to relax and lengthen tired, growing muscles.

## Flexibility
- Most climbing moves don't need extraordinary flexibility, but fast-growing bones and muscles can cause a growing sense of tightness that may somewhat limit movement on the rock.
- A moderate amount of daily stretching helps keep movement smooth and flexible.
- Gentle stretching of the arms, torso and legs belongs in a more comprehensive warm-up before maximal climbing.
- 10–15 minutes of stretching each evening (e.g. while watching TV or during quiet time before bed) is highly effective at relaxing and lengthening tired, growing muscles.
