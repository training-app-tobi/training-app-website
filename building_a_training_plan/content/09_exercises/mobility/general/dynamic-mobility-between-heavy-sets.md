# Dynamic Mobility Between Heavy Sets

**Type:** Exercise/protocol, Principles · **Trains:** Mobility · **For:** in-season

**TL;DR:** Put mobility inside the strength session, right after each heavy set, because stretching assigned to the end of a workout gets skipped. Keep it dynamic (inchworms, arm circles, dynamic hip and leg drills), since static stretching within a session or right before explosive exercise can hold back the gains; it is a compromise that keeps the habit and the progress, or stops older climbers losing flexibility with age.

## Mobility set
- In an integrated strength workout (the note Integrated Strength: Heavy Lifts, Finger Strength and Mobility in One Session), each circuit is one heavy strength exercise, then one mobility exercise, then one finger strength exercise, done three times.
- The mobility set comes after the heavy strength set and should be dynamic: inchworms, arm circles, dynamic hip and leg flexibility drills, rather than sitting on the floor doing the splits or stretching the arms in a doorway.
- When 15 minutes of stretching and mobility were assigned at the end of a one-hour workout, athletes finished the weight training and found they had no time left for the stretching; so the stretching was moved inside the session.
- **Warning:** Research shows that static stretching within a session, or right before explosive exercise, can hold back the gains from those exercises.
- Training is about finding what works for the athlete, not the ideal, so it is a compromise: when shoulder or hip flexibility training is put inside the session, people do it more often and keep the habit, so they keep progressing in mobility, or, as older climbers, keep from regressing and losing flexibility with age.
