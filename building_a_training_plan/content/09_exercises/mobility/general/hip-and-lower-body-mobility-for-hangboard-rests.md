# Hip and Lower-Body Mobility for Hangboard Rests

**Type:** Exercise/protocol · **Trains:** Mobility

**TL;DR:** Fill the rests of a hangboard routine with climber-specific mobility, so the forearms recover while you do something useful: a side split against a wall with lateral trunk flexion, high-feet drills with bent and straight knee, and Nordic hamstring curls (with padding under the knees); in the 3-minute rests of the last round, runner's and pigeon stretches, deep squat to hamstring stretch, standing side splits, and frog and child's pose.

## Rest exercises
- These fill the rests between hangs of a four-round hangboard routine (the note Hangboard Routine to Combine With Climbing on the Same Day): true active rest, so forearms and fingers recover while you work on hip and lower-body mobility for high feet, big reaches, better heel hooks and keeping the hips closer to the wall.

| Round | Exercise | How | Dose |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Side split against a wall with lateral trunk flexion | Helps balancing on wide feet and moving out to the side. Face a wall in a side-split position with the toes against the wall, arms out to the sides; reach the right hand toward the right toe while the left arm goes overhead as if reaching for a hold; return and repeat to the left | 5–6 reaches each side, holding 5 seconds at end range |
| 2 | High-feet drills, bent knee | Against a balcony (preferred) or wall, toes touching it. Lift one leg as high and as close to the body as you can, knee bent; return. Works hip mobility and high feet | Hold 2–3 seconds; 6 times each leg |
| 2 | High-feet drills, straight knee | Same position. Knee straight, reach one leg up and out to the side while keeping the hips close to the wall, without leaning the shoulders/chest to the side to compensate. Works hip mobility and high feet | Hold 2–3 seconds at end range; 5–6 times each leg |
| 3 | Nordic hamstring curls against a wall | Builds hamstring strength, warms the tissue for climbing afterwards, helps prevent injury, and improves body awareness (hamstrings, foot/ankle, glutes and core all engage). Kneel on the ground and back up against a wall, pulling the toes in (dorsiflexion) as you move back so as much of the foot as possible is on the wall (toes and heels touching it) | During the 90-second rest |
| 4, rest 1 | Runner's stretch and pigeon stretch | Variations: add rotation by opening the chest away from the front leg, or go deeper by bringing the chest toward the ground, even down to the elbows. Then drop the leg down into pigeon | During the 3-minute rest |
| 4, rest 2 | Deep squat to hamstring stretch | Deep squat wider or narrower; hamstring stretch with straight-leg and bent-leg variations | During the 3-minute rest |
| 4, rest 3 | Standing straight-leg and bent-leg side splits | Both variations matter. Go wider as you feel comfortable; reach leg to leg for an extra stretch | During the 3-minute rest |
| 4, rest 4 | Frog pose and child's pose | Further open the hips on top of the previous stretches; helps keep the hips closer to the wall while climbing | During the 3-minute rest |

- **Warning:** Padding under the knees is mandatory for Nordic hamstring curls.
- The stretches during the Round 4 rests (both versions) are mandatory. Use a 3-minute timer, or a 1-minute timer restarted 3 times (1 minute for plank bird dog, 2 × 1 minute for the stretches, switching sides or switching stretches).
