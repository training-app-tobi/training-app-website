# Three Styles of Bouldering Session

**Type:** Session, Planning · **Trains:** Endurance, Power endurance, General strength, Power, Technique, Mobility

**TL;DR:** Bouldering can train three things: conditioning, strength and power, or skill. Instead of a session that ends up as a mix of all three, separate them into discrete sessions for better results. Measure conditioning with a V-sum and density, measure strength and power by your average V-grade, and build skill by working on a couple of weaknesses for a 4–6 week training cycle.

## The three session types

| Session | Goal | How to do it | How to measure it |
|---|---|---|---|
| Conditioning: capacity | Capacity (endurance, strength endurance or capacity, whatever you call it): overall capacity for work in the gym | Climb in the bouldering gym for 60 or 90 minutes and keep track of the V-grade of every problem you complete | V-sum: add up the V-grades for a total at the end, e.g. 150 or 175 total V points; V0 counts as V1, so that it counts as something |
| Conditioning: anaerobic capacity | Anaerobic capacity | Compress as many V-grades as you can into a given time | Density = V-sum ÷ minutes; the greater the density, the better |
| Strength and power | Raise your average V-grade from session to session | Climb problems above your last average; rest plenty, focus and try hard | Average V-grade per session |
| Skill | Get better faster | Keep your mind in the movement; work on mobility, mindset and anti-style | A couple of chosen skills, worked on for a whole training cycle of 4–6 weeks |

- Most climbers just go in and have a session that ends up as a mix of all three; separating them into discrete sessions gives better results.
- Each session type has its own note: Bouldering Conditioning Session: V-Sum and Density; Bouldering Strength and Power Session: Raise Your Average Grade; Bouldering Skill Session: Mobility, Mindset and Anti-Style.
