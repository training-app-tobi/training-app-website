# Bouldering Conditioning Session: V-Sum and Density

**Type:** Session · **Trains:** Endurance, Power endurance

**TL;DR:** For capacity, climb in the bouldering gym for 60 or 90 minutes and add up the V-grades of every problem you complete (a V-sum, e.g. 150 or 175 total V points; V0 counts as V1). For anaerobic capacity, track density = V-sum ÷ minutes: the more V-grades you compress into a given time, the better, and quantifying it lets you see your endurance improve.

## Conditioning session

- Capacity: climb in the bouldering gym for 60 or 90 minutes, keep track of the V-grade of every problem you complete and add them up for a total at the end (a V-sum), e.g. 150 or 175 total V points. V0 counts as V1, so that it counts as something. This builds your overall capacity for work in the gym.
- Anaerobic capacity: measure density = V-sum ÷ minutes.

| V-sum | Time | Density |
|---|---|---|
| 90 V points | 90 minutes | 1 |
| 90 V points | 45 minutes | 2 |

- The more V-grades you compress into a given time, the greater the density and the better. Quantifying it lets you see your endurance improve.
