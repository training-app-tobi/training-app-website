# A Flexibility Session: Box Split, Butterfly, Hip Flexor Stretch and High Step

**Type:** Session, Exercise/protocol · **Trains:** Mobility

**TL;DR:** Static and dynamic flexibility training are both important: dynamic work trains movement through full ranges with higher sport specificity, static work increases overall mobility to the extremes. Train only when properly warmed up, ideally up to 5–7 days a week: 4 sets each of box split, butterfly (30 s reps), hip flexor stretch and high step (2 reps of 30 s), stretching to mild discomfort, not pain, and extending holds up to 90 s once you move on from 30-second maintenance reps.

## Flexibility session
- Static and dynamic flexibility training are both important for mobility and performance.

| Type | What it does |
|---|---|
| Dynamic | Increases movement through full ranges of motion; higher sport specificity |
| Static | Increases overall mobility to the extremes and joint range of motion |

- Train flexibility only when properly warmed up; ideally up to 5–7 days a week, for all sport-specific joints.
- **Warning:** Stretch to the point of mild discomfort, not to the point of pain.

4 exercises, 4 sets each.

| Exercise | Dose |
|---|---|
| Box split | 4 sets of one 30-second rep |
| Butterfly | 4 sets of one 30-second rep |
| Hip flexor stretch | 4 sets of 2 reps of 30 seconds |
| High step | 4 sets of 2 reps of 30 seconds |

- If you don't feel stable in a stretching position, ask a friend to help support you.

| Stage | Hold time |
|---|---|
| Maintenance | 30 s reps |
| Once you move on from 30-second maintenance reps | Extend holds up to 90 s for longer-lasting improvements |
