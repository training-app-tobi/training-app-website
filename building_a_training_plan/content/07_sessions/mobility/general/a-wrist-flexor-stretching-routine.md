# A Wrist Flexor Stretching Routine: Where Each Stretch Type Fits

**Type:** Session, Exercise/protocol · **Trains:** Mobility

**TL;DR:** A routine for the wrist flexors that shows where each stretch type fits around a climbing or hangboard session. Before the exercise: a warm-up of tendon gliding and putty squeezes (2x10 each hand), dynamic stretches held 1–3 seconds, then ballistic flicks with no hold. After it: PNF stretching (7–15 s contractions, 10–15 s relaxations) and static stretches of 3x30 seconds. You don't have to do every part; it varies with goals.

## Wrist flexor stretching routine
- Shows where each type of stretch (described in the note Stretching for Flexibility: Types, Timing and Dose) can fit in a session. You don't have to do every part; it varies with goals.

| Order | Part | What to do |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Warm-up | Tendon gliding 2x10 each hand; putty squeezes 2x10 each hand |
| 2 | Dynamic stretching | Lightly stretch the wrist flexors, holding each position only 1-3 seconds, moving through varying angles of stretch |
| 3 | Ballistic stretching | Repeat the dynamic stretches with quick isolations and no hold; do finger flicks and/or shake the fingers out |
| 4 | Exercise | Climbing, hangboarding, etc. |
| 5 | PNF stretching | Stretch the wrist flexors and fight it with a contraction for 7-15 seconds, then relax and immediately increase the stretch beyond the initial passive stretch. Relax 10-15 seconds, then repeat |
| 6 | Static stretching | 3x30 seconds, working into as much mobility as you can |
