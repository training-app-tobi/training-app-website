# Mobility Before a Bouldering Trip: Hide It in the Warm-Up

**Type:** Session, Planning · **Trains:** Mobility · **For:** preparing a bouldering trip

**TL;DR:** Keep mobility in the plan throughout, as a priority that helps compensate for weak small-hold performance. For a climber who doesn't like mobility training, hide it: build flexibility into the warm-up at the climbing wall in two sessions a week, and add a simple follow-along mobility routine at another point in the week.

## Mobility through the whole plan
- From a 12-week plan for a V9 boulderer's summer bouldering trip: the climbing sessions are planned first, then the fingerboard sessions, the weights-gym session and specific conditioning, which includes mobility.
- Mobility through the whole plan (a priority that helps compensate for small-hold performance). For a climber who doesn't like mobility training, hide it: build flexibility into the warm-up at the climbing wall in two sessions a week, so it is done at the start of climbing and helps performance through the rest of the session. A good way to improve flexibility if you don't like separate flexibility training.
- On top of that, a second, simple follow-along mobility routine at another point in the week, because mobility is a priority and there is time for it.
