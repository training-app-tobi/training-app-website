# Warming Up the Fingers on a Fingerboard

**Type:** Session · **Trains:** Finger strength · **For:** Beginner

**TL;DR:** Include a gentle fingerboard warm-up for the fingers from the very beginning: feet on the floor, slump bodyweight into half crimp and drag (open) grips, 5 s hangs over 4 to 8 reps, building until one or two hangs feel intense, then move on. It isn't training: it gets you used to the fingerboard and gets the muscles recruited and warm; warm up the shoulders and upper body too.

## Warm-up: fingers
- Warm-ups are personal and can include any number of exercises, but include this from the very beginning: gently warm up the fingers on a fingerboard. Many climbers warm up the rotator cuff and the lower body but neglect the fingers, even though climbing stresses the fingers probably harder than any other part of the body.

| Element | Detail |
|---|---|
| Grips | Half crimp and drag (open), plus any other grip you want to learn and get used to |
| Position | Feet stay on the floor; slowly slump bodyweight into the hold, from really light to relatively intense |
| Hangs | 5 s each, over 4, 6 or up to 8 reps (8 for a slightly longer warm-up) |
| Build-up | Gradually build until one or two hangs feel intense/challenging, then move on |
| More intensity | Take the feet off the floor if needed to make it challenging (possible if you are more advanced with strong fingers) |
| Purpose | Not training: just getting used to the fingerboard and getting the muscles recruited and warm |

- Half crimp: thumb not engaged, a bend in the finger halfway between a really closed grip (full crimp) and a really open grip. It mechanically engages the forearm muscles very well, which is why it is a good grip to use.
- Other grips to warm up: open three-finger, front three, back three, and smaller edges for full crimp. Half crimp and open three-finger are a great place to start.
- Also warm up the shoulders and upper body (this also happens while climbing): there are many exercises and no specific principle, just get them warm.
