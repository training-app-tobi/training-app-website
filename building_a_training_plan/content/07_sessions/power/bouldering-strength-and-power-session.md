# Bouldering Strength and Power Session: Raise Your Average Grade

**Type:** Session · **Trains:** Power, Finger strength

**TL;DR:** Aim to raise your average V-grade from session to session: if your average was V4.5 last time, spend your time on V5 and above. Rest plenty, focus and try hard on the harder problems; watching only the average grade keeps you out of the mentality of trying to get tired. Keep it simple.

## Strength and power session

- The aim is to raise your average V-grade from session to session.
- Watching only the average grade keeps you out of the mentality of trying to get tired and lets you focus on improving your bouldering.
- Example: if your average was V4.5 last time, spend your time on V5 and above to pull that number up.
- Rest plenty, focus and try hard: working on harder problems develops strength and power.
- Keep it simple; don't complicate it.
