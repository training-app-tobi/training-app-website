# Bouldering Skill Session: Mobility, Mindset and Anti-Style

**Type:** Session, Technique · **Trains:** Technique, Mobility

**TL;DR:** Deliberate skill work is a shortcut to getting better faster: keep your mind in the movement and notice what you are bad and good at. Work on mobility between climbs, practise the mindset of making yourself better rather than tired, and work on anti-style; pick just a couple of things for a whole 4–6 week training cycle.

## Skill session

- Skill builds whenever you climb, indoors or outside, even without paying attention to it, but deliberate skill work is a shortcut to getting better faster: keep your mind in the movement and pay attention to what you are bad at and what you are good at.
- Work on mobility in between climbs: make sure your hips and shoulders are mobile enough.
- Practise mindset: get out of the mindset of training to make yourself tired and into the mindset of making yourself better (like playing pool, where you wouldn't try to get tired).
- Work on anti-style: the things you don't find entertaining, the things you are bad at, or the things your peers are much better at than you. Those are the things to focus on.
- Pick just a couple of things and work on them for an entire training cycle of 4–6 weeks.
