# Skill Bouldering Grade Distribution

**Type:** Session, Principles · **Trains:** Technique

**TL;DR:** To develop skills better, boulder in the 75% range. Lay the session out on a normal distribution across zones of 55%, 65%, 75%, 85% and 95% of your max, so that most of the session is spent at 65–75% of max. A 75% problem is hard enough that you still have to use good skill and easy enough that you can still get a lot of volume in.

## Why boulder in the 75% range
- To develop skills better, boulder in the 75% range.
- When sessions are spent mostly on projects, always pushing against something that limits you, you don't spend enough quality time doing hard moves: the session ends up shortened, or there isn't enough stimulus to improve a skill.

## Distributing a session by % of max
- Lay the bouldering session out on a normal distribution across zones of 55%, 65%, 75%, 85% and 95% of your max (max = your maximum ability).
- Example with a hardest boulder problem of V9:

| Level | Example grade |
|---|---|
| Your maximum level | V9 |
| About 85% | V7 |
| 75% | V5 |
| 65% | V3 |
| 55% | V2–V1 and all the easier problems |

- Set up the session to distribute across this continuum, so that most of the session is spent at 65–75% of max (V3–V5 in the V9 example), which feels pretty easy.

## Why it works
- A 75% problem (V5 in the example) is hard enough that you still have to use good skill and easy enough that you can still get a lot of volume in.
- So even though the session doesn't feel as hard as you're used to, you develop skills and practise climbing at a higher level.
