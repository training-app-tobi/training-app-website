# Session Priority Order: Top Priority First

**Type:** Principles · **Trains:** General strength, Power, Finger strength

**TL;DR:** Do the session's top priority first, with full energy, then the second priority, and so on: that is why supplemental training goes after climbing, while a small, low-volume dose of strength work at the start is fine as activation. Do true low-rep power workouts before the session and high-rep workouts after, and don't do climbing power work after a fatiguing, powerful lifting session.

## Session order: weights before or after climbing
- Some people prefer weight training before climbing, some after; some climb at night, some in the morning: do what feels best for you. Supplemental training is programmed after climbing; some people do it the next day.
- How to order a session (unless you have consistent evidence that another method works better for you): the first stimulus is the top priority, the session's main intention, done with full energy, full mental strength and capacity, full psych and good quality. Then do the second priority with the remaining strength, then a third, and so on: the most attention and strength go to the top priority, the next most to the second. This is why supplemental training goes after climbing.
- A small amount of strength training at the start of the session as muscular activation is fine: below a dose and volume that would deliver an adaptation stimulus, done to prime the system and keep things recruited, even briefly at high intensity. If you kept progressing it, even that small dose could bring some adaptations, but the goal is only to get the session going.

| Warm-up set | Reps and intensity |
|---|---|
| First sets | High reps at low intensity (not super high: around 10 reps) |
| Each following set | Drop the volume and increase the intensity |
| On boulders | More V0s, V1s and V2s than V4s, V5s and V6s |

| Workout | When |
|---|---|
| True low-rep power workout | Before the session |
| True high-rep workout | After the session, unless it is the main goal of the session |

- **Warning:** Don't do climbing power work after a very powerful lifting session if the lifting was fatiguing. If you feel great and primed after it and the lifting was low volume, it can work (it has been programmed for people who find it works best for them).
