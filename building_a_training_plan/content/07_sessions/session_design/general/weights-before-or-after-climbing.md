# Weights Before or After Climbing

**Type:** Session, Principles · **Trains:** General strength, Finger strength, Technique

**TL;DR:** Whether strength exercises (e.g. hangboard, pull-ups, weights) go before or after climbing depends on the goal of the session. To build strength in muscles and tendons, do exercises first and climbing second; to focus on climbing and technique, climb first and do exercises second.

## Which order for which session goal
| Session focus | Order | Why |
|---|---|---|
| Building strength in muscles and tendons to supplement climbing | Exercises first, climbing second | You have full strength to perform the exercises |
| Climbing and technique | Climbing first, exercises second | You have more reserves of strength for climbing and technique |

## Other factors
- Whether you tend to fall early in or late into a problem you are working also guides whether to train strength when fresh (before climbing) or fatigued (after climbing).
- The right order varies with your needs and with the stage of the training cycle you are in.
