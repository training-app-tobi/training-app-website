# Power Training When Power Is a Weakness

**Type:** Principles · **Trains:** Power

**TL;DR:** If power is a weakness but not your top priority, do the power strength training after climbing, once the other things are done. Extremely low-intensity work, such as handless slab climbing and some mobility, can go at the end as long as you are not very tired.

## Power training when power is a weakness
- Do the power strength training after climbing if it is not your top priority: do the other things first.
- Exception: extremely low-intensity work, such as handless slab climbing and maybe some mobility, can go at the end as long as you are not very tired: it still needs good energy so you can do it at the highest quality possible.
