# A Weights-Gym Session Before a Bouldering Trip

**Type:** Session, Exercise/protocol · **Trains:** General strength, Pulling strength, Antagonists & core · **For:** preparing a bouldering trip

**TL;DR:** At least one weights-gym session a week alongside the climbing, kept simple: if something already works, keep it consistent and progress the lifts you already do over the 12 weeks, 4 to 5 lifts per workout. Deadlift, rack pull and overhead squat (rack pull only, heavier, in the build phase), pull-ups from strength to max strength, low row, and bench press moving to an incline dumbbell chest press.

## Gym session
- From a 12-week plan for a V9 boulderer's summer bouldering trip: the climbing sessions are planned first, then the fingerboard sessions, the weights-gym session and specific conditioning.
- At least one session a week at a weights gym alongside the climbing sessions. Keep conditioning simple. If something already works for you, keep it consistent until you start to see a decline: use the lifts already done and progress them over the 12 weeks; 4 to 5 lifts in the workout.

| Lift | Progression and purpose |
|---|---|
| Deadlift | First: big compound movement, same sets and reps as already done |
| Rack pull + overhead squat | The rack pull works trunk and core in a range of motion that applies to climbing and allows higher loads than a deadlift; the overhead squat works stability and strength overhead and still works a good range for legs and trunk |
| Rack pull only | Final mesocycle (build phase): increased load at really high intensity, to maximise body tension |
| Pull-ups | From a strength stimulus to max strength |
| Low row | For the mid-back and pulling into steeper terrain |
| Bench press → incline dumbbell chest press | Pushing. Dumbbells instead of a barbell bring a lot of stability work, and it works the anterior deltoids and upper chest more, which applies to climbing positions |

- Complex lifts such as deadlift and overhead squat suit a climber who can already lift with proper form, enjoys it, has seen it help their climbing and notices it on the wall when lifting is reduced. Not everyone has to do this type of conditioning.
