# Managing Water Retention During the Menstrual Cycle

**Type:** Injury & health, Planning · **Trains:** Finger strength · **For:** Female

**TL;DR:** Water retention can change weight within the menstrual cycle by up to 2 kg, which is noticeable when trying to access max strength. During the water-retention phase, drop the fingerboard load rather than pushing to match the previous session. Make deliberate choices about what to climb in this phase: lower-angle climbing, quick redpoints, or less fingery holds.

## Water retention and weight
- Water retention is very common during the menstrual cycle, and weight can vary within the cycle by up to 2 kg.
- It doesn't sound like much, but the difference is noticeable when trying to access max strength.

## Fingerboard training
- During the water-retention phase, the extra weight is a relatively large proportion of the max hang load used in fingerboard training, so drop the fingerboard load during this phase.
- Tracking makes you aware of when this is needed.
- **Warning:** pushing hard in this phase to match the previous session's load has left fingers feeling tweaky. Dropping the load avoids over-working the fingers.

## Climbing
- Make deliberate choices about what climbing to do during this phase, for example lower-angle climbing, quick redpoints, or a focus on less fingery holds. This has a positive effect on mentality and motivation.
- If you want to get on a project during this phase, be aware of the phase, and if the harder sections don't feel good, work on making the easier sections efficient.
