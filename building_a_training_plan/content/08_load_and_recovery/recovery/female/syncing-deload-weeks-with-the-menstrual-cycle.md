# Syncing Deload Weeks With the Menstrual Cycle

**Type:** Planning · **Trains:** Recovery · **For:** Female

**TL;DR:** Align deload weeks with the phase of the menstrual cycle where higher training volumes and/or intensities feel less accessible or enjoyable, often the late luteal phase or menstruation. Stay flexible within the deload week: change the focus of a session when intensity isn't accessible, and schedule alternate sessions so you can pick the one that fits how you feel on the day.

## Aligning deload weeks with the cycle
- Align deload weeks with the phase of the menstrual cycle where higher training volumes and/or intensities feel less accessible or enjoyable. This varies between people, but is often the late luteal phase or menstruation. It is one of the changes people find most positive, alongside tracking the cycle.
- A deload week is a time of reduced training (volume and/or intensity) so that you feel fully recovered and motivated to start another training cycle.
- Not every cycle is the same, so the training or climbing that feels possible in a deload week may also change from cycle to cycle. Being flexible is key to optimising training, climbing and the experience of both.

## Be flexible during deload weeks
- Be flexible by changing the focus of a session or the session itself. This is not "failing" or going "off plan", but an important part of listening to your body.

## Change the focus or intention of a session
- Deload weeks do not mean "doing nothing": many climbers still keep some harder sessions. If the intensity doesn't feel accessible when you start the session, still complete it but change its focus.

| Session | If the intensity isn't accessible | Change |
|---|---|---|
| Board session (keep 1) | You feel sluggish after warming up | Drop the grade and focus on climbing pace or body tension |
| Conditioning session | The load doesn't feel accessible | Drop the load and add visualisation alongside the movements |

## Plan alternate sessions
- Schedule a session together with an alternate session, so you can check in with how you feel on the day and choose the session that feels most accessible or motivating. Pair either a climbing session with an alternate conditioning session, or different types of climbing session.

| Planned session | Alternate session | Use the alternate when |
|---|---|---|
| Bouldering session | Weights session (quick to do and simpler movement-wise) | Coordination and muscle synchronisation feel off |
| Bouldering session | Lower-angle boulders-on-the-minute session | You are motivated to climb but don't feel like trying as hard |
