# Disrupted Sleep in the Late Luteal Phase

**Type:** Injury & health, Planning · **Trains:** Recovery · **For:** Female

**TL;DR:** Be flexible when scheduling the week so you aren't trying to complete high-intensity sessions on poor sleep; setting the late luteal phase as a deload week helps. If sleep has been disrupted, choose climbing that requires less coordination and simple conditioning exercises. Sleep habits that help are in the note Sleep Habits: Caffeine, Hydration and a Cool Room.

## Training
- Look at your schedule: be flexible when scheduling the week so you aren't trying to complete high-intensity sessions on poor sleep. Setting this phase as a deload week helps, as there are fewer sessions to fit in.
- Choose sessions accordingly: if sleep has been disrupted, climbing that requires less coordination and simple conditioning exercises may be less frustrating.
