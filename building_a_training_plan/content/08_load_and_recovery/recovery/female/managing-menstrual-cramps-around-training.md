# Managing Menstrual Cramps Around Training

**Type:** Injury & health, Planning · **Trains:** Recovery · **For:** Female

**TL;DR:** Know your cycle length and timings so you can predict when cramps are likely and be prepared. If you are prone to cramps, plan a deload or rest week to coincide with them, and plan those few days gently.

## Planning around cramps
- Know your cycle length and timings, so you can predict when cramps are likely and be prepared.
- If you are prone to cramps, plan a deload or rest week to coincide with them, and plan those few days gently ("through compassionate eyes").
