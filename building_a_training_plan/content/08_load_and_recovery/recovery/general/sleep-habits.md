# Sleep Habits: Caffeine, Hydration and a Cool Room

**Type:** Injury & health · **Trains:** Recovery

**TL;DR:** Cut back on caffeine and avoid it in the afternoon (its half-life is around 8 hours, so a coffee after lunch could still hinder sleep), drink plenty of water during the day but not close to bed, and keep the room cool to prevent overheating in the night.

## Sleep habits
- Watch caffeine intake: cut back on caffeine and try not to have it in the afternoon. The half-life of caffeine is around 8 hours, so a coffee after lunch could still hinder sleep.
- Hydrate at the right time: drink plenty of water throughout the day, but not close to bed, so you are hydrated but don't need to get up in the night.
- Keep it cool: try to keep the room cool to prevent overheating in the night.
