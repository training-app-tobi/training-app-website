# How to Take a Deload Week: Half Intensity, Half Volume

**Type:** Planning · **Trains:** Recovery

**TL;DR:** A deload week is about 50% of normal intensity and 50% of normal volume: go light, climb a couple of easy climbs for an hour at most, every other day, maybe with an extra rest day. The aim is to keep the stimulus alive and the muscles awake without tiring them at all: never leave the gym tired, leave feeling better than when you arrived.

## Deload week

| Deload | What to do |
|---|---|
| Week | About 50% of normal intensity and 50% of normal volume. Never leave the gym tired: leave feeling better than when you arrived |
| Session | Go light: a couple of easy climbs, flow around, have fun, like someone who doesn't push themselves at all. An hour at most, then leave |
| Frequency | Every other day, maybe with an extra rest day |
| Aim | Keep the stimulus alive and keep climbing, keeping the muscles awake without tiring or fatiguing them at all |
