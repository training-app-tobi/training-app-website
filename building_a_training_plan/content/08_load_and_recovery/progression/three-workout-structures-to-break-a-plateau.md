# Three Workout Structures to Break a Plateau

**Type:** Planning, Principles · **Trains:** General strength, Finger strength · **For:** stalled progress

**TL;DR:** Most climbers do some resistance training, hangboarding and bouldering but still fall into ruts in the tempo of movement, the volume of training or the order of exercises. Three workout structures aimed at different adaptations (strength aerobic training, static-dynamic finger rolls and contrast loading) can shake this up; none is revolutionary, but they tend to beat doing the same thing over and over.

## Why change the workout structure
- Most climbers do some resistance training, some hangboarding and bouldering, but still get into ruts in the tempo of movement, the volume of training or the order of exercises.
- Three workout structures, each aimed at a different adaptation, can shake up training. They are especially revealing when they work on something that is a limiter for you.
- The structures are in the notes Strength Aerobic Training and Contrast Loading, and Static-Dynamic Training: Barbell Finger Rolls for Forearm Endurance.

## Overall
- None of these methods is revolutionary or a reason to scrap what you're doing, but they are effective and tend to produce better results than doing the same thing over and over.
