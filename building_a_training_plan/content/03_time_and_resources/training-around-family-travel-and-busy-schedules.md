# Training Around Family, Travel and Busy Schedules

**Type:** Planning · **Trains:** General strength, Recovery · **For:** climbers with children or demanding careers

**TL;DR:** With children or a demanding career, energy is finite, so consistency and flexibility decide success: drop rigid expectations and use the time you do have. When time is short, do brief 20-minute home sessions, little and often, in nap times or other quiet windows, and superset basic exercises to save time. Strength detrains more slowly than most climbers fear: a week or two away from structured training often brings a recovery benefit.

| Situation / level | Do this |

## Training around family, travel and busy schedules
- Climbers with children or demanding careers: energy is finite; success depends on consistency and flexibility. You are more likely to stay consistent and progress long-term if you drop rigid expectations and use the time you do have.
- Supersets and clusters: combine basic exercises (e.g. superset your dumbbell work) to save time.
- Holidays and planned rest: strength detrains much more slowly than many climbers fear. A week or two completely away from structured training often brings a recovery benefit: ongoing connective tissue irritation settles, metabolic and CNS (central nervous system) fatigue resets, and motivation recharges.
