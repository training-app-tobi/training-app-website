# A 60° Board for Foot Tension in Volume Sessions

**Type:** Gear & boards · **Trains:** Technique

**TL;DR:** A Tension Board 2 at 60° is not a good idea for volume sessions to train foot tension: it is extremely hard, and the climbs would be very repetitive unless you are exceptionally strong.
