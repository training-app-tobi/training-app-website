# Performing in the Late Luteal Phase

**Type:** Goals & mindset, Injury & health · **For:** Female, late luteal phase

**TL;DR:** In the late luteal phase many women feel sluggish, distracted or anxious, but peak performance is still possible. Take a little longer to warm up and ease into the session, take more time going up the project bolt to bolt, and adopt a zero expectation mindset for redpoint attempts. If you need to perform during this phase, experiment in advance to find minor adjustments that help you work with your physiology.

## The problem
- In the late luteal phase many women feel their body is not primed for peak performance, and may feel a little sluggish, distracted or anxious.

## Take more time
- Take a little longer to warm up and ease into the session.
- On the project, take more time going up bolt to bolt to warm up on the moves and brush the holds.

## Zero expectation mindset
- Adopt a zero expectation mindset for redpoint attempts, even more so than normal. Useful phrases: "one move at a time" and "let's see what happens".

## It can work
- With this approach, a redpoint project was sent two days before the period, when symptoms are normally at their worst: peak performance is still possible in the late luteal phase.
- If a competition or trip falls in your late luteal phase, experiment in advance to find minor adjustments that help you work with your physiology.
