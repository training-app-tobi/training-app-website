## Training blocks, concurrent training and deloads
- Training blocks chunk the plan into individual periods, which may have a more distinct focus, e.g. building primarily strength or fitness.
- Most beginner to advanced climbers can see great success from a concurrent training plan: training several focuses at the same time. As long as your goals are focused and few, don't be concerned about interference between training methods until you reach a very advanced or elite level of performance.
- Deload week: every 3 to 4 weeks, at roughly 50% of the peak training load of that 3 to 4 week period.
