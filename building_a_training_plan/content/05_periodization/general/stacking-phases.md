**Type:** Planning · **Trains:** General strength, Power, Endurance

## Stacking phases in a training plan

- Whether the goal is bouldering, alpine climbing or a month-long sport trip, everything starts with the base. Most programming is planning which times of year to place a strength cycle and which to perform. With a good strength base, a specific conditioning cycle is built directly on top of low-volume strength training: the phases are not discrete, they are "stacked".

Example year:

| Period | Strength | Other focus |
|---|---|---|
| November–December | Build strength in the weight room and on the hangboard | |
| Next 3 months | Back off to 2 days per week of 20–30 minutes of strength work | Ramp up power and maybe anaerobic endurance |
| 2 more months | | Focus completely on sending |
| June | Back to a strength cycle | |
| Before the fall redpoint season | Another low-volume strength period | Develop power and conditioning |
