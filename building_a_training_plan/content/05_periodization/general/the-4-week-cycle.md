# The 4-Week Cycle: Ease In, Hardest in Week 3, Coast Into the Deload

**Type:** Planning · **Trains:** Power, Finger strength, Recovery

**TL;DR:** After testing new maxes, ease into power over about five sessions, starting with three easy ones: in effect a small deload after the testing. In any plan the first week is a reload, with volume and intensity well down even straight after a deload week. Push a little in week 2, make week 3 the hardest, and in week 4 don't chase an extra percent: coast into the deload. Repeat it every cycle.

## Easing in after testing
- With no trips or comps lined up, it's fine to enjoy peak strength and push power: push the ceiling and see where the max strength has got you. After max hangs and testing, go into power.

| After testing new maxes | Do this |
|---|---|
| Next three sessions | Easy, easing into power: the first power sessions shouldn't be all-out |
| In total | About five sessions of easing in, a small deload after the testing |

## The 4-week cycle
- In any plan, the first week is a reload: volume and intensity are dropped way down from the previous week, even straight after a deload week. You need to adapt and get up off the ground; going hard right away usually brings you right back down.

| Week | Do this |
|---|---|
| 1 | A good, guaranteed good week (eased in) |
| 2 | Push a little harder, just a little |
| 3 | The hardest week |
| 4 | Don't push, don't try to grab an extra percent, be happy with what you have: do another good week and coast into the deload |

- Repeat this every cycle.
