# A Year of General, Specific and Simulation Training

**Type:** Planning · **Trains:** General strength, Technique

**TL;DR:** Lay the year out as about 7–8 months of general training (while still doing some climbing and bouldering), about 3 months of specific training for goal climbs or personal limiters, and perhaps 1 month of simulation for a psyched climber, to turbocharge the chances of sending.

## The year
- General training develops overall energy systems and strength; specific training uses the sport's movement patterns or muscle groups and its loads and durations; simulation exactly mimics a route or competition setup.

| Phase | Duration | Content |
|---|---|---|
| General training | 7–8 months | General training while still doing some climbing and bouldering |
| Specific training | About 3 months | Specific adaptations for goal climbs or personal limiters |
| Simulation | Perhaps 1 month (for a psyched climber) | Simulation to "turbocharge" the chances of sending |
