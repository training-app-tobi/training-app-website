# Two Days On (Board Projects, Then Gym Volume) or Block Periodization

**Type:** Planning · **Trains:** Power, Finger strength, Endurance

**TL;DR:** Board projects on day 1, gym volume on day 2, then rest: you can keep this up indefinitely and keep seeing results, but it isn't the fastest way to maximise either. It works when the projects are less physical and more technical and the volume isn't very challenging; with very high-intensity training and very fatiguing volume the two interfere, and doing the high-intensity form more often gives better results.

## Two days on (board projects, then gym volume) or block periodization
- The pattern: day 1 board projects, day 2 gym volume, then one or more rest days. You can do it indefinitely and keep seeing results, but it is not the most optimal or fastest way to maximize results in either.

| Content of the two days | Does the pattern work? |
|---|---|
| Very high-intensity training and very fatiguing gym volume | Not the best: the two forms of training are not very symbiotic. To get very powerful or execute very hard moves, you get better results by doing the high-intensity form more often |
| Easier, less physical and more technical projects, and gym volume that isn't very high or very challenging | Yes, because the two interfere less |

- This is theoretical: what works best depends on the program design and on how well you execute it.
