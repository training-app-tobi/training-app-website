# The 8-8-8 Plan: 12 Weeks for Long-Term Endurance

**Type:** Planning · **Trains:** Endurance

**TL;DR:** A 12-week endurance program aimed at a lasting improvement rather than a short peak: never be fatigued on routes that used to be hard, and get three or four good attempts in a day instead of one. The first 8 weeks build capacity, training you not to get fatigued while climbing; the final 4 weeks resemble typical endurance training, teaching the body to handle massively fatiguing climbing while keeping good movement. Endurance built over time lasts much longer than a few short, maximally fatiguing workouts.

## The 8-8-8 Plan

- A 12-week program aimed at a long-term improvement in endurance, rather than an endurance peak that only brings you back to the level you reached before. The aim is to never be fatigued on previously hard routes, and to have three or four good attempts in a day instead of one. It is split into three 4-week sections.

| Weeks | Focus |
|---|---|
| First 8 weeks | Build capacity: training you not to get fatigued while climbing |
| Final 4 weeks | Resembles typical endurance training: teaches the body to handle massively fatiguing climbing while keeping good movement and efficiency |

- **Warning:** endurance lasts much longer when the fitness is built over time; little happens within a few short workouts. A basic flaw of "fitness training" is seeking maximum fatigue as quickly as possible: when the goal is technical climbing in a state of increasing fatigue, maybe several times a day, 4 min of 20 s work / 10 s rest helps little.
