# Strength in Base Periods, Power 2–6 Weeks Before a Goal

**Type:** Planning · **Trains:** Finger strength, Power

**TL;DR:** Strength and power training both benefit boulderers and route climbers, but time them separately in your training cycles. Train strength in the base periods of the season: it takes the longest to bring adaptations and strongly affects later power training. Introduce power 2–6 weeks before the goal, depending on the athlete and training history, to get into peak physical shape. The phases are the same for boulderers and route climbers.

## Structuring strength and power
- Both forms of training benefit boulderers and route climbers, but strength and power should be timed separately, according to your training cycles.

| Phase | Training | Why / when |
|---|---|---|
| "Base" periods of the season | Strength | It takes the longest to bring adaptations and has a significant effect on later power training |
| Before a goal | Power | To get into peak physical shape; introduce it 2-6 weeks before the goal (depending on the athlete and training history) |

- The phases are the same for boulderers and route climbers.
- The strength and power boulder sets themselves, and how many sessions a week to do, are in the note Strength and Power Boulder Sets: 6×3 Hard and 4×3 Fast.
