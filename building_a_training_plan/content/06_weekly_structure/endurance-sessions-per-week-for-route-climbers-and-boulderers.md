# Endurance Sessions per Week for Route Climbers and Boulderers

**Type:** Planning · **Trains:** Endurance · **For:** Route climbers, Boulderers

**TL;DR:** A route climber training three times a week needs at least 2 sessions a week that work an element of endurance. A boulderer needs at least 1, and as a rough guide only 25–50% of a route climber's endurance training, typically at lower grades and slightly lower intensity.

## Endurance sessions in the week
| Situation / level | Do this |
|---|---|
| Route climber training three times a week | At least 2 sessions a week that work an element of endurance |
| Boulderer | At least 1 session a week; as a rough guide, only 25–50% of a route climber's endurance training, typically at lower grades (slightly lower intensity) |

- The endurance sessions themselves (aerobic capacity, regeneration, ARC) are in the note Training Climbing Endurance: Aerobic Capacity and Regeneration.
