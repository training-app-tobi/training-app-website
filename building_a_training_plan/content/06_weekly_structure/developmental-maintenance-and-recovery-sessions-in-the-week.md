# Developmental, Maintenance and Recovery Sessions in the Week

**Type:** Planning · **Trains:** Recovery

**TL;DR:** The weekly schedule is the foundation everything has to fit into: find where the essential training fits around your fixed commitments and where added training minutes will go as you progress. Label each session developmental (D), maintenance (M) or recovery (R) and give the week a few of each, e.g. D M D R D M; athletes who think only in developmental terms leave their improvement to luck.

## The weekly schedule
- Each week has a fairly fixed schedule (weekends, places you must be on Monday, can't train until 5 pm on Wednesdays). Find where the essential training for the week fits, and where added training minutes will go as you progress. The weekly schedule is the foundation everything has to fit into.
- Prioritize what you do each week to improve the things you most need to improve.

| Session type | Label | Purpose |
|---|---|---|
| Developmental | D | Trying to get better at a specific part of the sport |
| Maintenance | M | Doing the same as before to hold a level of fitness |
| Recovery / restorative | R | An activity that enhances recovery from the harder sessions |

- A week has a few developmental sessions, a few maintenance sessions and some recovery.
- Example week, sessions in order across the week:

| Session | 1 | 2 | 3 | 4 | 5 | 6 |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Type | D | M | D | R | D | M |

- **Warning:** Many athletes think only in developmental terms: going hard on the MoonBoard with friends on Tuesday until very fatigued, again on Thursday, and hoping to send the project on Saturday. Then the only way to improve is luck; plan your training.
