# How Often to Use a Training Board: Grip Overlap and the Best Uses

**Type:** Gear & boards, Principles · **Trains:** Finger strength, Power

**TL;DR:** Use a board for a max strength day or a power day and stay off it on the other days: it carries too much load, and using the Tension Board 2 almost every session overlaps one grip (incut, half-crimp, high-angle) and puts the A2 pulley and PIP joint at risk. Boards are great for max strength and power, good for plyometric and explosive drills, and not great for power endurance, where the fail point is the fingers rather than metabolism.

## Using the Tension Board 2 almost every session
- **Warning:** Using the TB2 almost every session, you will definitely get stronger until you break. It is not a good idea unless you are really diligent about avoiding overlap.
- If you have other tools, get on other styles of climbing: on one wall angle you don't develop about three quarters of the sport's skills, wall angles and dimensions.
- **Warning:** Grip overlap: the TB2 has a lot of incut, half-crimp and high-angle gripping, so you overlap in one grip. The concern is the A2 pulley and the PIP joint.

## How often to use the board
| Goal | Boards |
|---|---|
| Max strength | Great |
| Power | Great |
| Plyometric drills | Good |
| Explosive power | Good |
| Power endurance | Not great |

- Why not power endurance: power endurance is metabolic, the stamina to put out high-quality output, and it needs many repeated efforts. On a board the fail point is usually the fingers (finger fry), a structural load on the soft tissue, while power endurance is after metabolic adaptations, not structural ones.
- Use the board for a max strength day or a power day, depending on your goal, and stay off it on the other days: it carries too much load.
- Easier climbing or skill work on the board can be done, but deliberately: choose the bigger holds and grip open-handed. Example: an athlete who boards every day does skill drills on the board on his skill days.
- Use the best tool for the goal you are after, not a tool rigged to do something it wasn't designed for.
- **Warning:** Board climbing every day: there are ways and a compromise if you have to, but you shortchange yourself and shouldn't do it. Nobody who exclusively uses the board is known to have reached the highest level of the sport.
