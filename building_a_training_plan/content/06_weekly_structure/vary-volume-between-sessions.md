# Vary Volume Between Sessions: One 20% Longer, One 20% Shorter

**Type:** Planning · **Trains:** General strength, Power

**TL;DR:** Avoid a rut of identical sessions (every bouldering day 2 hours, every strength day 30 minutes): regular, identical overloads get the body into a rut. In a week of two bouldering sessions plus performance climbing at the weekend, make one session about 20% longer and the other about 20% shorter. Total training stays the same, but the weekly variation can restart adaptations. It takes discipline, because the short day always feels like leaving something on the table, which is often how you get yourself to recover.

## Weekly programming
- **Warning:** Avoid falling into a rut of identical sessions (e.g. every bouldering day 2 hours, every strength day 30 minutes): those regular, identical overloads get the body into a rut you don't want.

In a week of two bouldering sessions plus performance climbing on the weekend, make one high-volume and one lower-volume session:

| Session | Duration |
|---|---|
| Tuesday (high volume) | Increase by 20% |
| Thursday (lower volume) | Reduce by 20% |

- Total training stays the same, but the weekly variation can restart adaptations and improve strength or overall bouldering ability.
- This takes discipline: on Tuesday you will feel you are pushing really hard, on Thursday you will always feel you left something on the table. Leaving something on the table is often how you get yourself to recover when you don't want to.
